### The idea

Vol targeting: measure trailing realised vol, scale gross so ex-ante vol
hits a target. Turnover targeting: measure trailing realised turnover,
adjust the trading-cost knob so expected turnover hits a **budget**
$\tau^*$. Then, whatever the true cost is, the book trades a known
amount, and net Sharpe at any cost level is gross minus a known drag.
The sign can no longer flip inside a plausible cost range because the
turnover is pinned.

Two knobs, one controller each:

- **Band variant** (changes the live construction): the band width
  $b_t$. Cube-root law says $b \propto c^{1/3}$, so turnover responds to
  $b$ with elasticity near −3 at small widths; the controller acts on
  $\log b$.
- **Joint variant** (inside P1.7's construction): the cost coefficient
  $c_t$.

### The controller, derived not tuned

Let $\hat\tau_t$ be the trailing 63-session realised turnover (annualised,
one-way, the harness definition), $\tau^*$ the budget. Update once per
session:

$$\log b_{t+1} = \log b_t + g \left(\log \hat\tau_t - \log \tau^*\right)$$

(same form for $c$). The gain $g$ is derived from the measured
turnover-to-width elasticity $\epsilon = \partial \log\tau / \partial\log
b$ (measure it from the band frontier table: turnover across widths 1.6%,
2.4%, 4.8%, 6.4%, 9.6%) and the measurement window $W = 63$: a
first-order controller with correction half-life equal to the window
has $g = -(1 - 2^{-1/W}) / \epsilon$. Show the arithmetic; do not sweep
$g$. Bounds: $b \in [2.4\%, 9.6\%]$ (the measured plateau's edges), $c \in
[5, 60]$bp. Log every update and every bound hit.

$\tau^*$: derive from the plateau, not from the argmax. The band frontier
shows net@15 flat from 4.8% to 9.6% (17.6 to 9.8×/yr) and net@30
positive only from 4.8% up. The budget is the turnover at the **lower
edge of the cost-robust plateau**, 17.6×/yr, which is also the live
band's expectation; state that it is a choice on the plateau and why the
lower edge (it keeps the most gross while never flipping sign at 30bp).

Start: $b_0$ = 4.8%, $\hat\tau$ seeded from the harness on the first 63
sessions of the sample (no lookahead: it uses only past turnover).

   under the controller the turnover is pinned regardless of the belief,

   fixed and targeted constructions **under each belief** {5, 15, 30}; the
   targeted column should be flat across beliefs. That flatness is the

4. **Stability of the controller itself:** plot $b_t$ over time; count

### Decision rule

Adopt the band variant into the live spec (`band_controller: {...}`
block, absent = fixed width, no-op proven) if test 1's stability
improves as stated, test 2's targeted column is flat within 0.1 Sharpe,
and gross at matched turnover is within 0.05 of the fixed band.
