## 0c. NEXT TASK ON capture_fills — unblock the cross-book dedup

The cross-book dedup fix is **deliberately not in `.4`**, and this is the work
that unblocks it. Read
`results/ops/CAPTURE_FILLS_CROSS_BOOK_2026-09-10.md` §5 first.

The short version: one execId is one broker execution with exactly one owner,
but there is no globally unique **order** key on disk to establish who that
owner is. `perm_id` is `0` on all 96 rows of the three `_order_map.csv` files,
and `(asof, order_id)` collides across books 8 times because IBKR order ids are
per-**clientId** sequences and the three books use different client ids.
`(asof, order_id, instrument)` happens to be unique today and is **not** unique
by construction — it breaks the first day two books trade one ticker on one
date, which is exactly the case this is about. Do not adopt it.

The fix is upstream, in `_record_order_attribution`
(`src/deploy/broker/ibkr.py:1192`): record a real global key. Two candidates,
and they are not equivalent —

- **`client_id`** is known at placement and is a pure addition. `(asof,
  client_id, order_id)` is then unique by construction.
- **`perm_id`** is the right answer but IBKR assigns it *asynchronously after
  transmission*, which is why the existing line records `0`. Capturing it means
  waiting on an order-status callback — a **timing change on the order path**.
  Do not do that casually.

**The trap when you add the column:** `_order_map.csv` already exists with an
8-field header and `csv.DictWriter` appends by position. Adding a field
mid-file writes 9 values under an 8-name header. Migrate the header, or append
the new field **last** and prove the readers still parse — `_load_order_map`
uses `DictReader`, so a trailing extra column is tolerated but never declared.
Write the test with the change.

Two tests already pin the current state and **should start failing** when the
key lands:
`ops/tests/test_capture_fills_cross_book.py::test_the_order_map_cannot_key_attribution_across_books`
and `::test_perm_id_is_the_missing_global_key`.
