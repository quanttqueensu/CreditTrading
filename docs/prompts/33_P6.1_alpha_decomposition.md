### The question

The signal demeans z across all 17 names. When the whole muni group
richens relative to taxables, every muni reads RICH and every taxable
reads CHEAP, and the demeaning turns that into a short-muni/long-taxable
book. Nobody chose that trade or sized it; it is what falls out of one
mean. So the live book is a mixture of two economically different bets:

- **Within-group selection:** which muni is rich against the other munis;
  which PIMCO fund is cheap against the other PIMCO funds. Many names,
  same holder base, same tax treatment, same leverage mechanics: the
  cleanest expression of M1 (retail sentiment noise at the fund level).
- **Between-group timing:** munis as a class against taxables as a
  class. One bet, driven by muni/Treasury ratios, tax-loss seasons,
  leverage-cost shocks: M5, and it is where the borrow bill lives.

Nobody has measured how much of the edge each one carries, what breadth
each has, or how correlated they are. Without that, P6.2 (size the group
bet on purpose) cannot be designed, and neither can any group-specific
signal (P6.3, P6.4) be evaluated, because we do not know what baseline
each group's signal is competing against.

### The decomposition, exact

On each date $t$, with group $g(i)$ from `cef_universe.csv` and the
eligible set $E_t$ after the ADV screen:

$$z_i = \bar z + (\bar z_{g(i)} - \bar z) + (z_i - \bar z_{g(i)})$$

so the live demeaned signal $s_i = -(z_i - \bar z)$ splits into

$$s_i = \underbrace{-(\bar z_{g(i)} - \bar z)}_{\text{between}_i}
      + \underbrace{-(z_i - \bar z_{g(i)})}_{\text{within}_i}.$$

Properties to assert in code: $\sum_i \text{within}_i = 0$ **within every
group** (the within book is group-neutral by construction);
$\sum_i \text{between}_i = 0$ (weighted by eligible counts, dollar-neutral);
$\text{between}_i + \text{within}_i = s_i$ exactly. A singleton group
(loan, JFR) has $\text{within} = 0$ and carries only a between component;
a group with fewer than 3 eligible names on a date is treated the same
way, and the number of such date-groups is reported.

### Three books, identical policy

Build target weights from each component exactly as the sleeve does from
$s_i$ (normalise to unit gross, vol-scale to 6% on the book's own
trailing 63-day realised vol, min-weight filter, band 4.8%), and score
with `evaluate()` at shift 2:

- **WITHIN**: from $\text{within}_i$.
- **BETWEEN**: from $\text{between}_i$ (every member of a group gets the
  same signed weight before normalisation; this is a group-spread book).
- **LIVE**: from $s_i$ (must reproduce the band's 1.16 / 17.6 / 0.66;
  regression check).

Also record, per date, the **implied mix** in the live book: the gross
share of the between component, $\sum_i |\text{between}_i| / \sum_i (|\text{between}_i| + |\text{within}_i|)$,
and its time series; expect it to be high today.

**3. Correlation and attribution.** Daily return correlation between

**4. Borrow by book.** The drag table (average short weight × fee) for

**5. The negative control.** Repeat the decomposition with **random
group assignments** (same group sizes, names shuffled, 20 draws). The
between component of a random grouping should have IC near zero and the
within component should recover the live IC. If a random between
component has an IC comparable to the real one, "group" is not doing
anything and the real between IC is a size or sector artefact.

**6. Group-by-group within IC.** The within IC computed inside each group
separately (muni: 6 names; multi: 8; hy: 2, which is a sign test). This
is the baseline every group-specific signal in P6.3 must beat.
