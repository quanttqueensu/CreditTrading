### The principle, restated so it is applied correctly

For each parameter, the cross-name spread splits into real signal and
estimation noise: $\tau^2 = \mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta) - E[se^2]$, and the
empirical-Bayes weight $w_i = \tau^2/(\tau^2 + se_i^2)$ is the share of
the observed spread that is real. The group $R^2$ says whether the
differences are structural. The measured table:

| parameter | mean $w$ | group $R^2$ | resolution |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| κ (reversion rate) | 0.86 | 0.12 | per-name |
| σ_d (discount vol) | 0.96 | 0.24 | per-name |
| σ_P (price vol) | 0.68 | **0.51** | **group** |

So κ and σ_d really differ by name (and are already inside the z-score,
which is why using them again lost), and price volatility is a *group*
property (munis 11–13%, multi 15–22%, HY 15–16%).

Apply per-name or per-group parameters **where they are absent** from
the construction: the policy layer (band width, holding period, cost)
and the risk scaling (price vol). Never in the score.
