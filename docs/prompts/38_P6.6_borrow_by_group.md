Munis carry 91% of the borrow bill on the current book. The short leg
of the between-group bet is, by construction, the muni complex. So the
cost of the group bet is not the muni discount's behaviour alone; it is
that behaviour minus 2–10% a year, and it is capacity-limited at
$45k–$278k in the two scarcest names.

### What to measure, as the panel accumulates

**1. Per group, per day** (from `cef_borrow.csv` × the current book and
× the frictionless target): weighted fee, pool depth in shares and in
dollars, our utilisation, drag $/yr, drag as % of the group's short MV.
A table for today and a time series that grows.

**2. Capacity by group.** Extend `borrow_capacity.py` to report, per
group, the capital at which the group's short leg is 25% unfillable
(φ = 0.25 of pool on the 95th-percentile short weight), and the
book-level capacity if the muni short leg is (a) as today, (b) capped
(P1.1), (c) expressed only in the group's two cheapest-to-borrow names.

**3. Net-of-borrow short alpha by group.** Using P1.2's $a_i$ with the
measured $H$ and $IC_H$: the mean expected short alpha per unit |z| for
each group after the fee. This says, per group, how rich a name must be
before shorting it is worth the carry. Expect: multi shorts profitable
from |z| ≈ 0.3; muni shorts in the scarce names only above |z| ≈ 1.5;
HYT never below |z| ≈ 1.5. Tabulate the breakeven |z| per name.

**4. The two questions for P6.2:**
- At what capital does the muni short leg stop being worth holding at
  all, i.e. where does the between bet's net-of-borrow Sharpe cross
  zero as availability forces the short into the expensive names?
- Is the between bet better expressed asymmetrically: long the taxable
  side fully, short only the cheapest-to-borrow munis (MHD 0.41%, MQY
  0.76%) with the rest of the muni exposure hedged by *not* being long
  taxables in the same size (i.e. a smaller, cheaper between book)?
  Compute both in the harness with borrow charged and hand the result
  to P6.2 as its last row.

**5. Fee dynamics, once there are 20+ days:** the correlation between a
name's fee change and its z change (the caveat in BORROW_NOTE: fees may
rise exactly when the short becomes attractive). Report per group.
