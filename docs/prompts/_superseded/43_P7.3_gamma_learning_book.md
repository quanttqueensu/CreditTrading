## Paste from here

You are building a small options book for the QUANTT team on the IBKR
paper account. The team asked "can we gamma scalp?" The honest answer,
recorded in `docs/prompts/00_README.md` Part H, is: mechanically yes, in
liquid ETF options; as a source of return, no — long gamma pays the
variance risk premium (SPY implied ÷ realised variance runs 1.3–1.5× in
`data/vrp/vrp_monthly.csv`) and wins only when realised exceeds implied.
This book exists so the team learns that by running it, measures it in
credit ETFs with real paper execution, and builds the option machinery
(greeks, rolls, hedging, the ledger) under the same discipline as the
strategy book. Read, in order:

1. `docs/prompts/00_README.md` Parts D, E, H.
2. `src/deploy/sleeve.py` (`OPTION`, `mark_fn`, `greeks_fn`),
   `src/deploy/exec_ledger.py::DerivativesLedger`,
   `src/deploy/broker/ibkr.py` (`_order`: options are `LimitOrder`;
   `_place_bag` for combos; `arm()` and the tag books), and
   `src/deploy/sleeves/null_trader.py` as the model of a book that exists
   to measure rather than earn.
3. `ops/books/phase0_book.json` and `ops/schedule/phase0.env` — how a
   separate book is declared, scheduled and given its own client id.
4. `data/vrp/costs_vrp.yaml` and its caveat; `atm_iv_daily.parquet`.

### Design

- **Book:** `ops/books/gamma_book.json`, sleeve `gamma_scalp`, capital
  $50,000, `max_gross_option_notional_usd: 50000`, own IBKR client id,
  own launchd job (`gamma`) at 17:35 so it never contends with cef 17:15
  (which waits for NAV) or benchmarks 17:25. Never netted with, or
  attributed to, any other book: shares it holds as a hedge are tagged to
  this book in `_attribution.json` and `arm()` must refuse if another
  book also trades the same ETF (HYG is held by the null trader and
  bench_b1 — so hedge in a name no other book holds, or give this book
  its own hedge instrument; state which).
- **Position:** one long ATM straddle at a time on HYG (credit) and one
  on TLT (rates), 30–45 DTE at entry, rolled at 14 DTE. Quantity from the
  cap, not from a view.
- **Hedge:** delta to zero at the close via MOC shares when |Δ| exceeds
  0.10 × the straddle's share-equivalent; otherwise hold. One hedge
  decision per session, same MOC discipline as everything else.
- **Greeks and marks:** Black–76 from the day's extracted IV (`atm_iv_daily`),
  never IBKR's model values. Option marks at the broker's mid at the
  close, recorded beside our model price so the gap is visible.

### The deliverable is the decomposition, not the P&L

Every session, decompose the book's P&L into

$$\underbrace{\tfrac12\Gamma S^2 r_t^2}_{\text{gamma}}\;-\;\underbrace{\Theta\,\Delta t}_{\text{theta}}\;+\;\underbrace{\mathcal{V}\,\Delta\sigma}_{\text{vega}}\;+\;\underbrace{\text{hedge P\&L}}_{\text{residual delta}}\;-\;\text{costs}\;+\;\text{unexplained}$$

and write it to the ledger as columns. The book is judged on **the size
of `unexplained`** (target: < 10% of gross gamma P&L over 60 sessions)
and on **60 sessions of clean, reconciled records** — not on whether it
made money. Gamma − theta is realised minus implied variance; the team
should be able to read that number off the dashboard and say which one
won that day.

### What it measures, for the programme

1. Realised vs implied variance in HYG and TLT, forward, session by
   session — extending `vrp_monthly.csv` into credit and rates with our
   own executions behind it.
2. Delta-hedge execution cost at MOC in ETF shares, in bp of hedge
   notional (adds to P1.3's cost programme).
3. **How kind IBKR paper option fills are:** fill vs our mid vs the
   extracted-IV model price, per leg. This number decides how much any
   live option result in this programme can be believed.
4. Whether the option ledger holds without desync across rolls,
   expiries and assignments (an ATM straddle at expiry is a real
   test of `DerivativesLedger`).

### Lifetime and rules

- Closes after 60 armed sessions unless P7.2 was adopted, in which case
  its machinery becomes the overlay's and this book is retired.
- Kill immediately on: any position in another book's ETF; any short
  option (this book is long-only in options by construction); notional
  above the cap; a desync.
- The short-straddle side of this trade is where the premium lives. It
  is deliberately **not** this book's job: it would add to the same
  stress exposure the strategy already carries (P7.1), and a $50k
  learning book is not where a short-vol programme gets decided.

### Deliverables

- `src/deploy/sleeves/gamma_scalp.py`, `src/deploy/lib/black76.py`
  (price, delta, gamma, theta, vega; unit-tested against put–call
  parity and against a handful of extracted marks), the book json, the
  `.env`, the launchd plist rendered by `ops/schedule/install.sh`, an
  `arm()` refusal test for the shared-ETF case.
- Dashboard: a **Gamma** tab, three tiles (gamma − theta today, implied
  vs realised 21d, unexplained %) and the decomposition table. Real
  data only; a session the book did not run shows as not run.
- `results/gamma/GAMMA_BOOK_<date>.md` at session 20, 40 and 60: the four
  measurements above and the decomposition accuracy.

### Do not

- Do not present this book's P&L as a strategy result anywhere.
- Do not size by conviction; the cap sizes it.
- Do not hedge in HYG if any other book holds HYG — attribution first.
