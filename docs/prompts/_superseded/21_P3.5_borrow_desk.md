**Lever:** the cost that trading less cannot reduce, made visible per name.

### Why a trader needs this on screen

- Borrow costs 1.22%/yr historically and **3.62%** on today's book,
  $13,732/yr, 2.75% of capital, because the signal has us short the whole
  Nuveen muni complex. That is more than half the strategy's expected net
  return, and it is not on any screen.
- Availability is a separate constraint. NAD's visible pool is 3,000
  shares against our 8,129 short. We hold it (it is borrowed), but a
  recall leaves nothing to re-borrow and we cannot add. A trader would
  want to know that before the signal asks for more NAD.
- Fees for CEFs move with the fund's own premium, which is the signal. A
  short that gets more attractive may get more expensive the same week.
  The panel is where that shows up first.

### The panel

**Per short name** (and every name in the universe, greyed when not
short):

| ticker | grp | fee % | rebate % | pool (shares) | our short (shares) | utilisation % | short MV $ | drag $/yr | recall risk | fee Δ 5d |

- utilisation = our short / pool; **crit above 25%** (φ), warn above 10%.
- recall risk: crit when utilisation > 100% (we hold more than the
  visible pool; NAD today), warn when pool < 2× our short.
- fee Δ 5d: change in fee over the last five rows for that name; "1 day
  of data" until there are five.

**Book totals**: short MV, weighted fee, drag $/yr, drag as % of NAV, and
the same four numbers for the **frictionless target** (from
`/api/signal`), so the trader sees what tonight's orders would do to the
bill. And the capacity line from `borrow_capacity.py`: "at $500k, 11.4%
of the desired short book is unfillable; binds first on NAD ($45k), NVG
($278k)".

**History**, once `cef_borrow.csv` has more than one date: a small
multiple per short name of fee % and pool over time. Never interpolate;
missing days are gaps.

### Server

`/api/borrow`: reads the latest date's rows from `cef_borrow.csv` and the
broker snapshot's positions. A name in the frozen universe with no row on
the latest date gets `fee: null, reason: "no rate on <date>"`; the totals
then carry `partial: true` and list the missing names. No median fill.
The frictionless-target totals use the signal frame's weights times NAV.

- Book drag matches BORROW_NOTE's live-book figure when computed on the

- With no borrow row for a name, the panel says so and the totals are

### Do not

- Do not compute a rate from the premium or any model; the panel shows
  what IBKR published.
- Do not feed any of this into the sleeve from the dashboard; P1.1 and
  P1.2 do that on the research path.
