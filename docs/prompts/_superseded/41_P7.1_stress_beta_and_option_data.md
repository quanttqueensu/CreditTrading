# P7.1 — Is the book short vol? Measure it, and get the credit-option data

**Lever:** none yet — this is the measurement that decides whether options
have any job in this book. **Trials:** 0. **Touches the live book:** no.
**Gates:** P7.2 (convexity overlay) and P7.4 (vol state). P7.3 does not
depend on it.

## Paste from here

You are a quant researcher on the QUANTT CEF book. The team has asked
whether gamma scalping — long options, delta-hedged, harvesting realised
against implied volatility — can be part of what we do. Before anyone
prices a straddle, answer the only question that decides it: **does this
book lose money in the states where long gamma pays?** Read, in order:

1. `docs/prompts/00_README.md` Parts A–D and Part H (options: what they
   can and cannot be here).
2. `scripts/cef/band_frontier.py` — `build_targets`, `band`, `evaluate`.
   `evaluate` returns summary statistics only; you will extend it to
   return the daily P&L series `pnl` (a visible extension, Part D rule 0:
   do not build a second backtester).
3. `docs/E1_PREREG.md` §5 — the factor set the previous book was tested
   against, including the **short-ATM-straddle proxy** built from
   `data/vrp/marks_SPY.parquet`. That regressor exists because a
   mean-reversion book can be "short vol in disguise": every position
   bounded, the portfolio still losing exactly when volatility jumps.
   It was run on the credit-RV book. It has **never been run on the CEF
   book.**
4. `data/vrp/` — what option data the repo already has: SPY/QQQ option
   trade marks 2014-06 → 2026-07 (`marks_SPY.parquet`, 389k rows), ATM
   implied vol by expiry (`atm_iv_daily.parquet`), VIX/VXN daily
   (`vix_daily.parquet`), and `vrp_monthly.csv`, whose `rich_ratio`
   (implied variance ÷ forward realised variance) has median 1.32 and
   mean 1.55 on SPY. Read the caveat at the top of `costs_vrp.yaml`: the
   option data is **trades-only**; no bid/ask exists; every option spread
   in this programme is an assumed floor.
5. `src/deploy/sleeve.py` — `OPTION` kind, `mark_fn` / `greeks_fn`: the
   option seam already exists in the ledger. Nothing in this prompt uses
   it; know that it is there.

### The mechanism, stated so it can be falsified

Discount reversion is liquidity provision to retail. Retail sells CEFs
hardest in credit sell-offs — 2008, December 2018, March 2020, the 2022
rates shock — and discounts widen *together* (the pairwise correlation
of daily discount changes is 0.42–0.66 across groups, Part F). The
z-score clips at ±4 and the vol target scales on 63-day realised vol,
which lags. So the book is already at full size when the shock arrives,
loses on the widening, and is paid back over the following weeks as
discounts revert. Whether that is "short vol" depends on the sign and
size of two things, and this prompt measures both:

- **β_vol**: the book's daily P&L loading on the *change* in implied
  volatility (ΔVIX now; ΔHYG-IV once extracted). Negative and
  significant means long gamma is a hedge for this book. Near zero means
  it is not, whatever the drawdowns look like.
- **Conditional IC**: the signal's 2-day/T+1 IC in high-vol versus
  low-vol states. If the IC is *higher* in stress, the book is "hurt by
  the shock, paid by the reversion" — a convexity hedge would be
  protecting the exact P&L the strategy is designed to earn, and the
  question becomes one of *timing* (P7.4), not protection.

### The measurements

1. **Extend `evaluate`** to return `pnl` (the daily series behind
   `gross_sr`). Run band 4.8% on the real panel; keep the pre-2013 flat
   convention and say so.
2. **Factor regression** of the daily P&L, 2014-01 → 2026-07 (the VIX
   window) and by era: HY excess return (HYG − duration-matched
   Treasury), IG excess, 10y Treasury return, SPX return, ΔVIX, the
   E1 short-ATM-straddle proxy from `marks_SPY`, credit momentum. Newey–
   West t-stats, lag 5. Report β, t, R², and the **β on ΔVIX in dollars
   per VIX point on the $500k book**. Repeat with ΔHYG-IV once step 5 is
   done.
3. **Drawdown coincidence.** The book's ten worst 21-session windows.
   For each: the VIX percentile at the start, the peak VIX inside the
   window, and the HYG-IV percentile. If eight or more of the ten sit in
   the top VIX quintile, the drawdowns are vol-state drawdowns. If they
   are spread across the distribution, they are not, and no option hedge
   addresses them.
4. **Conditional IC**: 2-day/T+1 IC (Part D rule 6) by VIX tercile at
   signal time, pooled and by group. Also the *forward* 21-day P&L of
   the book conditional on entering a top-decile VIX day.
5. **Extract the credit-option data.** HYG, LQD, TLT (and MUB if the
   source carries it) from the same trades source that produced
   `marks_SPY` (`data/vrp/_extract_SPY.log` shows the month-by-month
   pull). Build `atm_iv_daily` for each with the identical method and the
   same `n5_min` liquidity screen; report how many days survive the
   screen — MUB and LQD may not, and that is itself a finding about what
   can be traded. Extend `vrp_monthly.csv` so `rich_ratio` exists for
   credit as it does for equity.

### Decision (this prompt adopts nothing)

It produces four numbers and one sentence:

- β_ΔVIX (and β_ΔHYG-IV) with t-stat;
- the fraction of the ten worst windows in the top VIX quintile;
- the IC ratio, high-vol tercile ÷ low-vol tercile;
- the credit `rich_ratio` (median, by ticker);
- and the sentence: **"The book is / is not short vol at the portfolio
  level, because …"**

**P7.2 is gated on β_ΔVIX < 0 with |t| ≥ 2.5 AND ≥ 7 of 10 drawdown
windows in the top VIX quintile.** Either condition failing closes P7.2
before it opens: a hedge that pays in states where the book does not
lose is a pure carry cost, and the honest answer to "can we gamma scalp"
becomes P7.3 alone (a learning book) plus P7.4 (options as information).

### Do not

- Do not trade anything. Do not open an options book.
- Do not use IBKR model greeks or IBKR implied vols anywhere: they
  require a market-data subscription the paper account may not carry and
  they cannot be reproduced. Every vol in this programme comes from the
  extracted marks via a Black–76 inversion we own.
- Do not interpret a negative β_vol as "buy puts". It is the entry ticket
  to P7.2, where the carry is charged.
