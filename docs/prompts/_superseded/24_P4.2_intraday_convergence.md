## Paste from here

You are adding an intraday view to the QUANTT CEF dashboard. Read
`docs/EXIT_RESEARCH_2026-09-07.md` §3 and §5.2 in full. The asymmetry is
the whole point:

- **Entry cannot be intraday.** The signal is price minus NAV, and a
  fund's NAV does not exist until after the close. There is no intraday
  discount to compute for a new position. Structural, not a data gap.
- **Exit can be intraday.** A position we already hold has a known prior
  NAV. Price moves all day against it. A position whose price has
  converged toward yesterday's NAV has captured its move with no new
  information; the remaining expected return is smaller and the position
  is now cheap to exit.

Also read `results/cef/BORROW_NOTE_2026-09-06.md` "Source" (the account
has no NYSE top-of-book subscription, error 10089; the API works under
`reqMarketDataType(3)`, delayed 15 minutes) and `dashboard/broker_session.py`
(P0.3; one session, event-driven, P4.1).

### What to compute

For each name $i$ we hold, during RTH, from the delayed quote:

- $P^{now}_i$: delayed last (or mid of delayed bid/ask when last is
  stale),
- $N_i$: the last published NAV and its date,
- $d^{now}_i = 100 (P^{now}_i - N_i)/N_i$: the intraday discount against
  the known NAV,
- $z^{now}_i = (d^{now}_i - \mu_i)/\sigma_i$ using the **same** 252-day
  moments (shift 1) the signal uses, from the cached panel,
- $z^{sig}_i$: the z at the last signal date,
- **converged fraction** $= 1 - z^{now}_i / z^{sig}_i$ when
  $|z^{sig}_i| > 0.5$, else "n/a: no dislocation to converge"; clip to
  [−1, 2] for display and show the raw number on hover,
- intraday P&L of the position at the delayed price.

Every value carries the quote timestamp and the word DELAYED.
