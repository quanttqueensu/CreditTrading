### Why a calendar and not a signal

Distribution data failed twice as a *signal* because the damage from a
cut lands at 63 days and the book holds for 12. But an ex-date still moves
price and not NAV, mechanically, on a known day; a fund at a 9% yield
paying monthly drops ~0.75% on the ex-date and the discount reads 75bp
wider for a day. A tender offer at NAV moves a 10% discount to ~2% on a
known date. A trader wants these on the calendar so a move that looks
like dislocation is recognised as a date, and so a short on a name with a
tender pending is seen for what it is.

### The panel

**Next 10 sessions**, one row per day:

- NYSE holiday / early close (from the calendar module),
- ex-dates in the universe (ticker, amount, implied one-day discount
  sawtooth = amount / price in bp),
- names whose NAV routinely publishes late (from the NAV panel: the
  fraction of the last 60 sessions where the name's NAV date lagged the
  panel max),
- manual events.

**Manual events file** `ops/books/cef_live/events.yaml`, entered by the
team by hand, with a schema:

```
- ticker: NAD
  kind: tender | rights_offering | merger | open_ending | activist_13d |
        distribution_change | other
  date: 2026-10-15
  detail: "Saba 13D filed 2026-09-05; tender for 15% at 98% of NAV"
  source: "https://..."
  entered_by: initials
```

A name with an event inside the next 5 sessions is flagged on the
blotter (P3.1) with the kind. A short on a name with a `tender`,
`rights_offering` or `open_ending` inside 20 sessions is flagged crit
with the text "jump-to-NAV risk on the short leg".

**Forward ex-dates.** `fetch_cef_distributions.py` stages history;
extend it (or add `scripts/cef/fetch_declared_distributions.py`) to fetch
declared-but-not-yet-ex distributions from the fund families' pages or
CEFConnect, append to a forward file `data/cef/cef_declared.csv` with a
source column, and run it in the PANELS phase. Never infer an ex-date
from cadence; a monthly payer that skips is exactly the event a trader
needs to see.

### Server

`/api/calendar`: the ten-day table, the manual events (validated against
the schema; a malformed entry is reported as such, not dropped), and the
late-NAV list. Reads only.

### Acceptance

- Holidays and early closes for the next month match the NYSE calendar
  module (test).
- The ex-date list for the next ten sessions is non-empty (these are
  monthly payers) and each row's sawtooth is computed from the latest
  close.
- A test event in `events.yaml` for a short name inside 20 sessions flags
  the blotter row.

### Do not

- Do not turn any calendar item into a sleeve input from the dashboard.
- Do not fabricate a future ex-date from the previous cadence.
