### The nowcast anchor (modelled; label it on every figure)

$\widehat{NAV}_{t,\tau} = NAV_{t-1}\,(1 + \hat\beta' r^{ETF}_{t,\tau})$ where
$r^{ETF}_{t,\tau}$ are the proxy ETFs' returns from yesterday's close to
intraday time $\tau$ and $\hat\beta$ is fitted on the trailing 252 days of
**daily** NAV returns against the same ETFs' daily returns, ending *t−1*.
Report the daily-frequency $R^2$ of that regression first — for PDI/PTY
expect 0.5–0.8. The intraday nowcast error is then at least
$\sqrt{1-R^2}$ of the NAV's daily vol; convert that to discount points and
compare it with the intraday discount moves you intend to trade. If the
nowcast error is of the same order as the signal, stop and write that; the
strategy cannot see what it is trading.

### The signal and the test

Intraday discount $d_{t,\tau} = P_{t,\tau}/\widehat{NAV}_{t,\tau} - 1$.
Anchor: the trailing 20-session mean of the *daily* discount (the same
object the daily signal uses, at a shorter window because the horizon is
hours; state it, do not sweep it). Deviation $e_{t,\tau} = d_{t,\tau} -
\bar d_{t-1}$.

Enter when $|e|$ exceeds $k \cdot \hat\sigma_e$ with $\hat\sigma_e$ the
trailing 20-session intraday standard deviation of $e$ and $k = 2$ (chosen
now; report 1.5 and 2.5 as sensitivity); exit when $e$ crosses zero or at
the close, whichever first; one position per name at a time; fills at the
next bar's mid **minus** the measured half-spread from BID_ASK bars, plus
an impact charge of $\sqrt{q/ADV_{5min}}$ bp scaled to 10bp at 10% of the
5-minute volume. Cap participation at 1% of 5-minute volume.

Report, by name and year: round trips per day, gross edge per round trip
(bp), cost per round trip (bp), the E1 ratio (edge ÷ cost), net Sharpe at
1×/2×/3× measured spread, and the fraction of P&L that survives if the
nowcast error is doubled (shock $\hat\beta$ by its standard error). Also the
**time-of-day** profile of the edge: if it lives only in the first and last
half hour, that is a different (and known) effect — the open/close
liquidity premium — and should be named as such.

### Decision rule

Proceed to a *separate* intraday paper book pre-registration only if, on
both names, on the full sample **and** the 2023–26 holdout: E1 ratio ≥ 2.5
at 2× spread, net Sharpe ≥ 1.5 at 2× spread, and the result survives the
doubled nowcast error with at least half its Sharpe. Otherwise record and
stop; the daily book keeps MOC and P4.2 stays display-only. Under no
outcome does this prompt change the live sleeve.
