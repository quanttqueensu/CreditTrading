The emd group is a **fifth kind of fund** with its own driver (EM
sovereign and corporate spreads, currency), a holder base that overlaps
with high-yield retail, and NAVs that may lag because of foreign
holdings and time-zone pricing. It is not in the live book at all. HY
and loan are thin in the 17 (2 and 1 names) and rich in the 27. The
additions that raise effective breadth are the ones that make the book
less than 92% one factor, and those are non-muni by construction.

### Task

**1. Eligibility from P1.6's tick screen**, per date, for all 27. Report
each name's median half-spread bp, ADV, NAV age distribution (emd names
in particular: what fraction of sessions is the NAV a day late?), and
borrow fee if a row exists in `cef_borrow.csv` (extend
`fetch_borrow_rates.py` to pull the 27 too; it is one file).

**2. Candidate sets, group-balanced.** For target sizes 3, 5, 8 names,
construct the set by a stated rule, not by search: rank eligible
non-muni names by median half-spread ascending, take them in order
subject to at most 2 per group per step so the additions are spread
across hy, loan, multi and emd. Also the muni-inclusive comparison set
(same rule with munis allowed) so the reader sees the contrast.

**3. Evaluate** each set as the 17 plus the additions, live policy
unchanged, harness turnover-matched to 17.6×/yr:

| set | n | median eligible/day | gross | turn/yr | net@15 | net@30 | net of borrow | BR_eff | PC2 share | mean half-spread bp | emd names' NAV-lag days/yr |

**4. The frontier.** Plot BR_eff against mean half-spread bp for every
set; the promotion is the point where BR_eff stops rising faster than
cost.

**5. emd specifically.** Because it is a new factor, check: the IC of
the live signal on the five emd names alone (from HOLDOUT27's per-group
IC if reported; else compute), their NAV timing, and whether their
addition changes PC1's reading (a sixth factor could appear as a new
PC2). If emd NAVs are routinely a day late, they fail `max_nav_age_bd`
and are excluded honestly.

### Decision rule

Promote the smallest set that raises BR_eff by ≥ 1.0 and lowers PC2
share by ≥ 15 points at matched turnover, with net@15 and net@30 not
worse than the 17 alone and the additions' median half-spread no worse
than the live median. Pre-register
(`results/cef/PREREG_BREADTH_GROUPS_<date>.md`): the names, the rule
that chose them, expected BR_eff and PC2, the falsifier (BR_eff below the
17-only book after 20 sessions, or any added name failing NAV age on
more than 10% of sessions). Update `UNIVERSE` in the spec and in
`band_frontier.py` together. Trials +1.

Record that promoting names consumes the 27 as a comparison set; the
remaining names are the new holdout, listed.
