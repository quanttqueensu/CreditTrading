### What gamma scalping is, in one identity

Hold an option, hedge its delta continuously, and the daily P&L is

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\,\Gamma S^2\left(r_t^2 - \sigma_{\text{imp}}^2\,\Delta t\right)$$

— gamma P&L minus theta, which is **realised variance minus implied
variance**, scaled by dollar gamma. Delta hedging removes the directional
bet; what remains is a bet that the underlying moves more than the
option price assumed. In the repo's own SPY data implied variance runs
1.3–1.5× realised (median/mean `rich_ratio`), so a systematic long-gamma
position **loses on average**. It can still be worth holding if its
payoff arrives in the sessions the book loses, and by enough. That is a
portfolio-level question and it is answered by Calmar and the tail, not
by the overlay's own Sharpe, which will be negative.

### Construction (state each choice before running)

- **Underlying:** the credit ETF with the largest |β| from P7.1 — HYG
  expected; LQD and TLT as alternates. Options must clear the
  `n5_min` screen on ≥ 90% of days or the underlying is untradeable for
  this purpose.
- **Structure, primary:** 25-delta puts, 1–3 month tenor, rolled at 21
  DTE. Puts, not straddles, because the book's losses are one-sided:
  discount widening is a credit sell-off. **Secondary:** ATM straddles,
  reported alongside, not selected on.
- **Sizing by theta budget**, not by contracts: 0.5%, 1.0%, 2.0% of NAV
  per year. The notional follows from the budget and the day's IV, so the
  hedge automatically shrinks when vol is expensive — the opposite of
  buying most when protection costs most.
- **Delta hedge:** daily, at the close, in ETF shares, MOC, in the same
  session as the CEF book (the executor already batches). Hedge band
  ±0.05 × notional in delta terms so the hedge does not churn on noise.
- **No short options anywhere.** Selling a call to "finance" the put
  re-introduces the tail the overlay exists to remove.

### The backtest

Daily, 2014-06 → 2026-07 (the IV window), on top of band 4.8%'s daily
P&L from `band_frontier`. Option marks from the extracted trades, Black–
76 greeks from the extracted IV; charge the option spread floor at
1×/2×/3× on every leg crossing (entry, exit, roll = two legs each) and
the hedge at 0.5bp half-spread per rebalance. Report for the book alone
and book + overlay at each budget:

| metric | pre-committed role |
|---|---|
| net Sharpe (15bp on the book, spread floor on the options) | must not fall by more than 0.05 |
| **Calmar** (ann. return ÷ max drawdown) | primary: must rise ≥ 25% |
| worst month, 5 worst months | must improve in ≥ 4 of 5 |
| skew of monthly returns | reported |
| overlay carry, %/yr | reported; this is what the hedge costs |
| overlay P&L in the book's 10 worst windows (P7.1 table) | reported; this is what it buys |

Bootstrap the Calmar difference (block bootstrap, 21-day blocks, 2,000
draws) and report the 5th percentile; adopt only if it is positive.

**Negative control:** the identical overlay on SPY puts. If SPY protects
the book as well as HYG does, the hedge is generic equity tail
protection, not credit convexity. It may still be adopted; it is named
correctly and the underlying with the better Calmar-per-carry wins,
stated before the run.

### Decision rule (fixed before running)

Adopt the **smallest** budget at which, at **all three** spread
multipliers: Calmar rises ≥ 25%, net Sharpe falls ≤ 0.05, the five worst
months improve in ≥ 4, and the bootstrap 5th percentile of the Calmar
change is > 0. If no budget passes, record the table and stop — the
answer to "gamma scalping" for this book is then P7.3 and P7.4 only.
Never pick the budget on Sharpe or on the overlay's own return.

Pre-register `results/cef/PREREG_CONVEXITY_<date>.md`: underlying,
structure, budget, hedge band, the table above with the thresholds, and
the sentence "the overlay's own P&L is expected to be negative; it is
judged on the book's Calmar". Trials +1.

### Deployment, if adopted

- A separate sleeve `cef_convexity_overlay` in `cef_discount_book.json`,
  its own sub-ledger, `OPTION` legs via `LimitOrder` at mid ± 1 tick,
  greeks from our Black–76 on the day's extracted IV, marks from the
  broker's mid at the close. The hedge leg is ordinary MOC shares.
- Risk keys in the frozen spec: `theta_budget_pct`, `max_option_notional_usd`,
  `vega_limit_usd`, `no_short_options: true`. New keys default to "no
  overlay" and the no-op is proved by diffing the CEF sleeve's output
  with the keys absent.
- Reported separately and combined on the dashboard. The main book's
  kill rule is unchanged and is evaluated on the book *without* the
  overlay, so the hedge can never mask a failing strategy.
- **Paper realism:** IBKR paper fills options generously (mid, no dealer
  layer). Live option fills are not execution evidence; the hedge fills
  (shares, MOC) are.

### Do not

- Do not hedge intraday. There is no intraday process in this system and
  MOC is the only fill we can measure.
- Do not scale the budget with recent losses. That is buying protection
  after the loss.
- Do not report the overlay's Sharpe as if it were the result.
