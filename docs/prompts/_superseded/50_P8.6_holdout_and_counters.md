### Why the 27 alone is no longer enough

The live universe is the liquid 17 (Part I). The 27 are the illiquid tail
($0.1–2.4m ADV against the 17's $3.1–49m). A specification that works on
both liquidity regimes is reassuring, but a specification that works on
the 17 and not the 27 may simply be a liquidity-conditional effect, and
one that works on the 27 and not the 17 is untradeable. Sign agreement on
the 27 is a *weak* check. A **time holdout** on the same names is a strong
one: it asks whether the rule, fitted through 2022, still works after.

### The rules

1. **Time holdout.** Every quantity that is *estimated* — a window length,
   a threshold, a coefficient, a band width, a cost model, a factor loading
   — is estimated on data **before 2023-01-01**. Every harness table shows
   two rows: fit period (2005–2022) and holdout (2023–2026), never a pooled
   row alone. Derived quantities (the cube-root band width from the law,
   Σ⁻¹·IR budgets) are computed on the fit period and *applied* to the
   holdout unchanged. Rolling estimators (252-day z moments) are fine on
   the holdout because they use only trailing data; what is forbidden is
   *choosing* anything with sight of 2023–26.
2. **The 27 stay** as the second check under P1.6's HOLDOUT27 rules, run
   once, before the 17, recorded first. A decision rule requires the
   holdout row to agree in sign and the 27 to agree in sign; if they
   disagree with each other, the spec is not adopted and the disagreement
   is written up (it is information about liquidity-dependence).
3. **Two counters** in `docs/RESEARCH_STATE.md`:
   - `CEF trials: 48` — every specification of the discount book (signal,
     policy, universe, sizing) evaluated with sight of P&L, including
     P8.5's intraday test.
   - `GAMMA trials: 0` — every specification of the options sleeve.
   Each has its own deflated-Sharpe bar √(2 ln N). A prompt says which
   counter it increments. The **combined** book is judged on the joint
   record, not on either counter alone.
4. **The falsifier is pre-registered with the trial.** Every adoption names
   the live statistic and the horizon that would reverse it (P1.4's
   turnover readout is the model).
