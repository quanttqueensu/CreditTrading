### What happens on a lag day

The sleeve wants HYT at +0.0761 (band HOLD). The shadow ledger cannot price
HYT on the sizing date, drops it from `desired`, then sweeps it to zero: a
modelled SELL of 4,598 shares, about $38k, charged the modelled half-spread.
The live broker, correctly, sends nothing. The next day HYT's close arrives,
the sleeve still wants +0.0761, and the shadow ledger books a BUY of ~4,598
shares. Net effect in the P&L source: a phantom round trip of ~$76k, 15% of
NAV, with modelled costs, on a day the strategy did nothing. The ledger and
broker then disagree by 4,598 HYT shares until the next `arm()` reseeds,
and the dashboard's reconcile panel shows a "material break" that nobody
caused.

This corrupts exactly the two numbers the pre-registration says we will
judge the band on: realised turnover and cost.
