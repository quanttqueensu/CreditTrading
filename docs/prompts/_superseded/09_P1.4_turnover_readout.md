### Definitions, fixed so the live number and the backtest number are the same object

- **Session turnover** $\tau_t = \sum_i |q_{it} P_{it}| / N_t$ from
  **broker-confirmed fills only**, where $q_{it}$ is the filled quantity,
  $P_{it}$ the fill VWAP, $N_t$ the sleeve NAV at decision. Modelled
  sessions do not count; sessions that did not arm do not count as zero,
  they are excluded from the denominator (a non-armed day is a missing
  observation, not a quiet one).
- **Annualised turnover** $= 252 \cdot \bar\tau$ over armed sessions,
  with a moving-block bootstrap CI (block 5 sessions).
- **Strategy turnover** excludes dust orders (any order whose reason is a
  band HOLD; after P0.1 there are none) and phantom flattens.
- **Holding period**, two estimates reported side by side: (i) mean gross
  / mean session turnover, the harness definition; (ii) per-position life
  from first fill to flat, from `positions.csv`, median and mean.
- **Counterfactual calendar-2d turnover on the same dates:**
  `calendar(T, 2)` from `build_targets()` restricted to the armed session
  dates, so the comparison is same-days, same-signal, policy-only.
- **Counterfactual band turnover** the same way, `band(T, 0.048)`, which
  is what the live book *should* be doing if the implementation is right.
  The live number should track this one; a gap between them is the
  implementation, not the market.
