### The confound that must be removed before anything is tested

PIMCO funds pay large **year-end supplemental distributions** in
December. An ex-date drops the price and not the NAV, so the discount
reads mechanically wider on the ex-date and "recovers" as the next
months' price drifts. A December widening / January narrowing in the
multi group could be nothing but ex-date arithmetic. So:

- exclude ex-date days and the day after from every seasonal mean (from
  `cef_distributions.parquet`), and
- report each seasonal mean **with and without** ex-date days, so the
  reader sees how much of it was mechanical.

Same treatment for all groups (munis pay monthly; the exclusion is
small there).

### Pre-registration: write `results/cef/PREREG_SEASONAL_<date>.md` BEFORE running anything

Commit to:

**Hypotheses, one per group, with the mechanism:**
- **muni:** tax-loss selling widens discounts in Oct–Nov (retail sells
  losers for the deduction; muni CEFs are ~100% retail-held) and they
  narrow in Nov–Jan. Prediction: mean discount change negative in
  Oct–early Nov, positive from mid-Nov through Jan. Mechanism check:
  December **volume** is elevated relative to the year in muni names, and
  the January narrowing is larger after years in which the group's price
  return was negative (more losses to harvest): a dose-response test.
- **multi:** December widening and January narrowing **survive the
  ex-date exclusion**. If they do not, the effect is distributions, not
  flow, and the hypothesis is rejected for this group. Second
  prediction: September widening (−9.6 in the rough pass) with no
  distribution story; mechanism unknown, stated as such, and a result
  without a mechanism is recorded but not adopted.
- **hy:** September widening. Same status as multi's September.
- **loan:** November narrowing. n = 1 in the 17; test on the six loan
  names in the 27 only.

**The test:** for each group and each hypothesised window, the mean daily
discount change in-window minus out-of-window, Newey-West t with 21
lags, on the **27 first**, then the 17, 2005–2026, and by decade. Also
the year-by-year sign so a single year cannot carry it.

**The decision rule:** a group's seasonal is adopted **only as a
conditioner in P6.3** (a window dummy in $\theta_{g,t}$), never as a
standalone overlay in the live book, and only if: p < 0.01 on the 27,
same sign on the 17, survives the ex-date exclusion, and the mechanism
check passes for muni (volume and dose-response). One trial for the
whole run.

**The January basis sleeve**, separately: recompute NICHE_EDGE §3's
hedged January basis **by group** (long the group's CEFs, short the
matched ETF basket: munis vs a muni ETF, HY vs HYG/JNK, multi vs a
HYG/LQD blend, at a rolling 252-day hedge ratio), with the same
robustness rows the July note ran (ex-2009, over-hedged, by era). Report
which group carries it. Do not deploy it in this prompt; if it holds by
group, it is a separate pre-registration for next January with its own
trial.
