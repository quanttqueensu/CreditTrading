### The three conditioners, declared here and not amended

Each produces a state $S_t \in \{0, 1\}$ at the close of *t*; the sleeve is
long an ATM straddle (HYG primary; TLT reported) delta-hedged at the close
while $S_t = 1$, flat otherwise. Entry and exit are MOC in shares and
limit-at-mid in options at the next session (shift 1 on the option, same
as the book's shift-2 convention once the hedge is included; state it).

**C1 — CEF stress nowcast (ours; the one nobody else has).** Retail
liquidation shows up in CEF discounts before it shows up in realised ETF
volatility, because the CEF holder sells the wrapper at whatever price and
the ETF's arbitrage keeps its price near NAV until flows are large. Build
from the 17, at *t*:

- $D_t$ = cross-sectional standard deviation of the day's discount
  *changes*;
- $V_t$ = mean over names of $(z_t - z_{t-3})$, the 3-session z-velocity,
  signed so that widening is positive;
- $W_t$ = fraction of names whose discount widened by more than one
  trailing-63-day standard deviation today.

Standardise each on a trailing 252-session window ending *t−1* and average:
$\text{Stress}_t = \tfrac13(\tilde D_t + \tilde V_t + \tilde W_t)$.
$S^{C1}_t = 1$ if $\text{Stress}_t > 1.5$ (one and a half standard
deviations, chosen before running; report 1.0 and 2.0 as sensitivity, do
not select). **The falsifiable claim, tested first as a regression:**
$\text{Stress}_t$ predicts $RV_{t+1..t+5}(\text{HYG}) - IV_t$ with a
positive coefficient, out of sample by era, and predicts it *better* than
$\Delta$VIX$_t$ does. If it does not, C1 is dead and the sleeve has nothing
of ours in it.

**C2 — Implied cheapness and term structure.** $S^{C2}_t = 1$ when the
1-month ATM IV is below the trailing 63-day realised vol (implied cheap
against recent realised) **or** the 1-month/3-month IV ratio exceeds 1.0
(inverted term structure, stress already priced at the front). Both from
`atm_iv_daily`. Known in the literature, crowded, weak — included as the
control C1 must beat.

**C3 — Event calendar.** $S^{C3}_t = 1$ from two sessions before to one
session after FOMC decisions, CPI and payrolls releases (dates from the
official calendars, fetched into `data/vrp/events.csv` with a source note).
Realised vol clusters on those days; whether implied under-prices it in
credit ETFs is the test.

### The backtest, one run

2014-06 → 2026-07 (the IV window), on HYG (and TLT reported), the P7.2
engine: option marks from extracted trades, Black–76 greeks, spread floor
1×/2×/3× of $0.02 per leg crossing, hedge at 0.5bp, daily delta hedge with
a ±0.05·notional band. For each conditioner and for the always-on control:

| | ann. return | vol | Sharpe | Calmar | time in market | trades/yr | carry while on (%/yr) | P&L in the book's 10 worst windows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then the **combined book** at the derived budget: with $IR_g$, $IR_c$ the
net Sharpes of the gamma sleeve and the CEF band book (net of borrow, 15bp)
and $\rho$ their daily correlation, $v \propto \Sigma^{-1} IR$ as in P6.2,
total vol 6% (the live target; P1.5 may raise it later). If
$IR_g - \rho IR_c \le 0$ the derived budget is zero and the sleeve is not
an engine — record it and hand the question back to P7.2 (hedge terms).

Report by era and on the **2023–2026 time holdout** separately (Part I: the
time holdout applies to every fit; the 1.5σ threshold and the 252-day
windows are fixed, so the holdout tests the *rule*, not a fit).

### Decision rule, fixed before running

Adopt **at most one** conditioner, and only if: its Sharpe is positive at
2× spread on the full sample **and** on the 2023–26 holdout; C1 must beat
C2 by ≥ 0.10 Sharpe to be chosen over it (the prior favours the known
effect unless ours is clearly better); the derived budget is ≥ 5% of book
vol (below that the tickets are not worth the ledger); and the combined
book's Calmar is not lower than the CEF book alone (absolute return is the
objective, but not by buying a worse tail). If C3 alone passes, the sleeve
is an event-window sleeve and is sized and named as such.

Pre-register `results/gamma/PREREG_GAMMA_<date>.md`: the conditioner, the
threshold, the underlying, the budget, the readout (realised-minus-implied
captured per on-day, hedge cost, decomposition accuracy from P7.3 over 40
sessions). **Gamma counter: 1.**
