### The principle

The live book holds the within-group and between-group bets in whatever
proportion one cross-sectional mean produces. Today that is ~90%
between. The fundamental law says each bet deserves capital in
proportion to its information ratio, $IR_k \approx IC_k\sqrt{BR_k}$, and
that two bets with correlation $\rho$ should be combined as a
mean-variance portfolio of sleeves, not summed.

Hard group neutrality is the special case "budget for between = 0", and
it lost. The live book is the special case "budget = whatever the
demeaning gives". Neither is a decision. This prompt makes it one.

### The construction

Two sleeves with identical policy (band 4.8%, MOC shift 2, min-weight,
ADV screen), each vol-targeted to its **own** budget:

- **WITHIN** sleeve: weights from $\text{within}_i$ (group-neutral by
  construction), vol target $v_w$.
- **BETWEEN** sleeve: weights from $\text{between}_i$ (a group-spread
  book), vol target $v_b$, **borrow charged by name** (this sleeve
  carries the muni shorts).

Combined book = the sum of the two weight vectors, then the band applied
to the combined target (so turnover is of the combined book; report also
the two sleeves banded separately, which is a different and slightly
worse construction, for reference).

**The budget split, derived.** Treat the two sleeves as assets with
Sharpe $IR_w, IR_b$ (use P6.1's measured *net-of-borrow* Sharpes at
matched turnover, not the gross), correlation $\rho$. The vol-space
weights that maximise the combined Sharpe are proportional to
$\Sigma^{-1} IR$ with $\Sigma = \begin{pmatrix}1&\rho\\\rho&1\end{pmatrix}$:

$$\begin{pmatrix} v_w \\ v_b \end{pmatrix} \propto
\frac{1}{1-\rho^2}\begin{pmatrix} IR_w - \rho IR_b \\ IR_b - \rho IR_w \end{pmatrix},
\qquad v_w^2 + v_b^2 + 2\rho v_w v_b = (6\%)^2.$$

Show the arithmetic with P6.1's numbers. If $IR_b - \rho IR_w \le 0$, the
between bet gets **zero** budget and the answer is group-neutral
construction; write that down and reconcile it with the 0.97 → 0.82
finding (different sample? different policy? borrow not charged then?)
before concluding.

**Do not sweep the split.** It is derived once from P6.1. Report a
sensitivity (±50% on $v_b$) for the reader, but the decision is at the
derived point.

### Decision rule

Adopt the two-sleeve construction if, at matched turnover, net@15 −
borrow improves over LIVE by ≥ 0.05 with net@30 not worse, BR_eff rises
by ≥ 1.0, and the improvement holds in at least four of five eras. If
WITHIN-only wins, that is the answer and the earlier neutrality finding
is superseded with the reconciliation written. If nothing beats LIVE,
record it: the accidental mix was near-optimal, which is itself a
finding.
