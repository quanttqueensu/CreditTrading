## Paste from here

You are a quant researcher on the QUANTT CEF book. P7.1 measured how the
signal's IC and the book's losses depend on the volatility state. This
prompt asks whether the options market — which prices tomorrow's
volatility today — should set two of the book's parameters that are
currently constants or lagging estimates. Read, in order:

1. `results/cef/PREREG_BAND_2026-09-06.md` — the band width is derived
   from the cube-root law $h^* = (3c\,\sigma_w^2 / 2\kappa_w)^{1/3}$ with
   $\sigma_w$ = 0.04083/day measured on the *whole sample*.
2. `src/deploy/sleeves/cef_discount.py` — the vol target uses 63-day
   realised vol, `clip(0.06 / rv_63, 0.2, 2.5)`.
3. `results/cef/STRESS_BETA_<date>.md` (P7.1) — conditional IC by VIX
   tercile; forward 21-day P&L after top-decile VIX days.
4. `data/vrp/atm_iv_daily.parquet` (HYG/LQD/TLT now present),
   `vix_daily.parquet`.
5. `docs/prompts/00_README.md` Part D — turnover-matched, cost grid, eras,
   the 27, no sweeping.

### The mechanism

Two of the book's parameters assume a stationary world:

- **The band.** $\sigma_w$ — how fast target weights move — is not
  constant: in a vol spike z-scores move several units in days and the
  targets with them. The cube-root law says the no-trade region should
  widen with $\sigma_w^{2/3}$; a constant 4.8% band is too narrow in
  stress (it trades every twitch at the worst spreads) and too wide in
  calm. Implied vol at decision time is a forward-looking estimate of
  $\sigma_w$'s driver; realised vol is a backward one.
- **The vol target.** 63-day realised vol lags a shock by weeks, so the
  book is at full size when the shock lands and *smallest* weeks later
  when the reversion pays (P7.1's conditional IC tells you whether that
  is when the edge is largest). A blend that leans on implied vol reacts
  on the day.

Both are **derived** changes: the functional form comes from the law
already in the pre-registration, and the only new input is a vol series
available at 17:15 on decision day. Nothing is swept.

### Constructions

1. **State-scaled band:** $h_t = 4.8\% \times (\text{IV}_t / \text{IV}_{\text{med}})^{2/3}$,
   IV = HYG ATM implied vol at decision time, median over the trailing
   504 sessions (trailing, so no lookahead), $h_t$ clipped to
   [3%, 9.6%] — the measured plateau's edges, stated in advance.
2. **Blended vol scalar:** replace `rv_63` with
   $\sqrt{\tfrac12\,\text{rv}_{63}^2 + \tfrac12\,\text{IV}_t^2 \cdot \beta^2}$
   where $\beta$ is the book's trailing 252-day beta of realised vol to
   HYG IV (so the IV is translated into the book's own vol units, not
   assumed equal). Same clip.
3. Both together.

Each run under the harness at the **turnover-matched** width or scalar
(Part D rule 2), cost grid 5/15/30, net of borrow, by era, on the 17 and
then once on the 27.

### The tests

- Conditional performance: net@15 and turnover in the top VIX tercile
  versus the bottom, for the live spec and each construction. The gain
  must sit in the high-vol tercile; a uniform gain fails the mechanism.
- **Negative control:** the same constructions driven by VXN (Nasdaq
  vol) instead of HYG IV. If VXN works as well, the input is "any vol
  index", and the credit-specific story is not the story; still may be
  adopted, but named as generic vol-state conditioning.
- Turnover stability by era (sd/mean) — a state-dependent band must not
  be less stable than 11.9%.

### Decision rule

Adopt a construction if: net@15 and net@30 improve at matched turnover,
the gain is concentrated in the high-vol tercile, the 27 agree in sign,
and era turnover stability is not worse. If two pass, the band-only
change wins by default (simpler; one parameter), stated in advance.

Pre-register `PREREG_VOL_STATE_<date>.md`: spec keys
`band_state: "const" | "iv_scaled"` and `vol_input: "realised" | "blend"`,
absent = current behaviour, no-op proved byte-for-byte. The HYG IV
series must be fetched into the panel by the same refresh that fetches
NAV — a decision input that is not in the panel at 17:15 is not a
decision input. Trials +1.
