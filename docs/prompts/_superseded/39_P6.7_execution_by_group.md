### Two sources of cost information, neither yet used by group

**From history: a daily spread estimate for every name since 1986.**
The panel has high and low. Corwin & Schultz (2012, JF) estimate the
bid-ask spread from the ratio of two-day to one-day high-low ranges:

$$\beta = \left[\ln\frac{H_t}{L_t}\right]^2 + \left[\ln\frac{H_{t+1}}{L_{t+1}}\right]^2,\qquad
\gamma = \left[\ln\frac{H_{t,t+1}}{L_{t,t+1}}\right]^2,$$
$$\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2\beta} - \sqrt{\beta}}{3 - 2\sqrt2} - \sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{3 - 2\sqrt2}},\qquad
S = \frac{2(e^\alpha - 1)}{1 + e^\alpha},$$

with negative estimates set to zero and a monthly average taken. This
gives a spread panel by name and by group across the whole backtest
sample, so the harness's flat 15bp can be replaced by a group- and
era-specific cost, and `costs.yaml`'s 2026 probe can be checked against
two decades of implied spreads.

**From our fills: the shortfall log** (P1.3), which has 31 real fills so
far and will grow by ~15 a session.
