# P3.4 — Order lifecycle and the session journal

**Lever:** a trader's view of what was sent, what filled, and at what
cost, as it happens. **Trials:** 0. **Touches the live book:** writes an
append-only log from the live path; changes no decision.

### What a trader wants to see at 17:20 ET

"Thirteen orders were decided at 17:15. Twelve transmitted; one was
skipped because it was unpriced. They rest as MOC until tomorrow's close.
Tomorrow at 16:00 they fill; by 16:05 I see each fill against the
decision close, and by 17:30 the journal says what the session did."

Today none of that is visible. Orders live in `orders.csv` with a status
column, fills arrive in `broker_fills.csv` after capture, and nothing ties
an order to its fill or shows the resting state.

### Task

**1. Order events, append-only.** In `ibkr.py::place_targets`, after each
`placeOrder`, and in an `orderStatusEvent` handler while the session is
connected, append to
`ops/books/cef_live/_ibkr_shadow/cef_discount/order_events.csv`:

`ts_utc, decision_date, ticker, side, qty, order_type, limit_price,
ib_order_id, perm_id, status (decided | transmitted | skipped_unpriced |
rejected | resting | partially_filled | filled | cancelled), filled_qty,
avg_fill_price, decision_close, reason`.

The live path writes `decided`, `transmitted`, `skipped_*`, `rejected`.
`capture_fills.py` writes `filled` / `partially_filled` with the VWAP and
links by `perm_id`. Idempotent on re-run: dedupe on `(perm_id, status)`.
Never rewrite a row.

**2. `/api/orders`.** Today's lifecycle: merge `order_events.csv` for the
current decision date with the broker snapshot's `openOrders` (resting
state, live) and `fills` (as they arrive). Per order: the state chain
with timestamps, and once filled, `exec_bp` against the official close
(P1.3's definition). Sessions in the past: from the file only.

**3. Screen 4 card "Tonight / Today".** Before the close: each order with
its state and a resting indicator. After the close: fills as they arrive
(the broker session pushes them), each with exec bp coloured by sign, a
session total, and the number still unfilled. Unfilled MOC after the close
is a red row: it means the auction did not fill it, which for a MOC is a
broker-side event worth a human.

**4. The journal.** At the end of every session (phase 4, after capture),
write `ops/books/cef_live/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md`:

```
# Session 2026-09-09 — cef_discount
armed: yes | no (blockers: ...)
phases: REFRESH ok 17:02 · PANELS ok 17:03 · PREFLIGHT ok 17:14 · TRADE ok 17:16 · CAPTURE ok 17:31
decision: band 4.8%, signal date 2026-09-08, volscal 1.51, 17 eligible
orders: n, gross $, by kind
fills: n, gross $, exec bp mean ± sd, delay bp (already in backtest)
provenance: broker_confirmed | modelled_only | no_trades
risk: gross, net, BR_eff, PC2 share, net muni weight
borrow: weighted fee, $/yr on the book
alerts: none | ...
notes: <anything the job printed at WARNING or above>
```

Written by a function in `ops/journal.py` that reads files only; if a
section's source is missing the section says so. Screen 2 shows the last
five journals in a drawer; `#journal/2026-09-09` deep-links one.
