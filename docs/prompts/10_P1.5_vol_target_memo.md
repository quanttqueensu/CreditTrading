### The arithmetic the memo rests on

- The **unit-gross book** (Σ|w| = 1) realises about 4% annualised vol
  today. A target $v$ needs gross $g = v / 0.04$: 6% → 1.5×, 9% → 2.25×,
  12% → 3.0×, 15% → 3.75×.
- **Reg T** caps gross at 2× equity, so at this vol Reg T allows a target
  of about 8%. Anything above needs Portfolio Margin.
- **Kelly.** For a strategy with Sharpe $S$ and normal returns, the
  growth-optimal vol target is $S$ itself (full Kelly). At the band's
  net-of-borrow $S \approx 0.43$–$0.48$ that is a 43–48% vol target; 6% is
  a Kelly fraction of ~0.13. Half-Kelly is ~22%; quarter-Kelly ~11%. With
  kurtosis 41.2 nobody should run above quarter-Kelly, and the fraction is
  a stated tolerance, not a derivation.
- **Borrow scales with gross.** Drag at 1.22%/yr on held short MV at gross
  1.5× is ~0.9% of NAV; at 3× it is ~1.8% of NAV. Return also doubles, so
  the ratio is unchanged, but the dollar bill is not, and the availability
  cap (P1.1) binds harder.
- **Expected return** at target $v$ and net Sharpe $S$: $S v$. At 6% and
  0.45: 2.7%/yr, $13.5k on $500k. At 12%: $27k.
