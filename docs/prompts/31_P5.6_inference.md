### What to add, each with its formula stated in the code

**1. Lo (2002) autocorrelation-corrected Sharpe SE.** For daily returns
with first-order autocorrelation $\rho$, the annualised Sharpe from $T$
observations has standard error approximately
$$SE(\widehat{SR}_{ann}) \approx \sqrt{\frac{1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\widehat{SR}_{d}^{\,2}}{T}}\cdot \eta(q),$$
where $\widehat{SR}_d$ is the daily Sharpe and $\eta(q)$ is Lo's
aggregation factor for $q = 252$ under the measured $\rho$ (implement his
equation for the $q$-period Sharpe with AR(1) autocorrelation; state it).
Apply to the live confirmed-session return series and to the backtest
path. Report the SE next to every Sharpe the dashboard shows, and refuse
to show a Sharpe without it.

**2. Minimum track record length** (Bailey & López de Prado 2012):
$$MinTRL = 1 + \left[1 - \hat\gamma_3 \widehat{SR} + \frac{\hat\gamma_4 - 1}{4}\widehat{SR}^2\right]\left(\frac{z_\alpha}{\widehat{SR} - SR^*}\right)^2$$
in the same frequency as the returns, with skew $\hat\gamma_3$, kurtosis
$\hat\gamma_4$ (41.2 on the backtest; measure the live one), and $SR^*$
the benchmark (0 for "is it positive"; 0.43 for "is it the band's
expectation"). Report MinTRL in sessions for the live book at
$\alpha$ = 0.05; it will be years. That is the honest statement of why
P&L is not the readout.

**3. Probability of backtest overfitting** (Bailey, Borwein, López de
Prado & Zhu 2014, CSCV): over the configuration set the band frontier
already evaluates (widths 0.2–12.8%, plus the calendars, at each cost
level), split the sample into $S = 16$ blocks, evaluate all
$\binom{16}{8}$ combinations of in-sample/out-of-sample halves, and
compute the probability that the in-sample-best configuration has
below-median out-of-sample rank. Report PBO at 5/15/30bp. Note in the
write-up that the band width was *derived* not selected, so PBO here
measures the risk we avoided, and it becomes the bar for any future
selected parameter.

**4. Deflated Sharpe ratio** (Bailey & López de Prado 2014): with $N$
= the CEF trial counter and the variance of trial Sharpes, the expected
maximum Sharpe under the null and the probability the observed Sharpe
exceeds it, with skew and kurtosis adjustment. Read $N$ from
`RESEARCH_STATE.md` automatically (parse the counter line), so the
number moves when the counter does. If per-trial Sharpes are not
recorded, reconstruct what can be reconstructed from the results notes,
list them in a table with sources, and state the assumption used for the
rest.

**5. The cost test's power**, since it is the kill trigger (P0.5): given
the observed per-session dispersion of `exec_bp` from P1.3's log, the
number of MOC sessions needed to detect a mean 10bp above breakeven at
80% power, and the current power at the sessions on hand.

### Where it goes

- `src/backtest/tearsheet.py`: functions `lo_se`, `min_trl`, `pbo_cscv`,
  `deflated_sharpe`, each with a docstring stating the formula and the
  paper, and a test against a hand-computed example.
- `scripts/cef/inference_report.py`: runs all five on the current live
  series and the harness path; writes `results/cef/INFERENCE_<date>.md`
  and `results/cef/inference.json`.
- Dashboard screen 2: Sharpe tiles show `value ± SE (n sessions; MinTRL
  N)`; the drawer shows PBO and DSR with their inputs and the trial count.

### Acceptance

- The live Sharpe tile never appears without its SE and n.
- The DSR recomputes when `RESEARCH_STATE.md`'s counter line changes
  (test by editing a copy).
- PBO on the band frontier's grid is reported at three cost levels with
  the number of configurations and blocks stated.

### Do not

- Do not compute any of these on modelled sessions.
- Do not tune $S$ or the block length to make PBO look better; 16 blocks
  is the paper's default and is stated in advance.
