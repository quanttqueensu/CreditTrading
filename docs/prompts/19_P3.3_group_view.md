### What the risk panel does not yet say

It says PC2 is 92.5% of variance and net muni weight is −74.9%. It does
not say the thing a trader needs: **the book is, in effect, one trade:
short the muni-CEF discount against the taxable-CEF discount, at some
level of that spread, with a 3.62% borrow bill attached.** Nobody sized
that trade; the demeaning produced it. The view should show it as a trade
so the desk can see whether the level is extreme, how it has moved, and
what it costs to carry.

### The view

**1. Group table** (also the subtotal rows of the blotter, P3.1):

| group | n | gross wt | net wt | mean z | mean discount | borrow $/yr | fee wtd | unrealised | share of book variance (from Σ) |

The last column: decompose book variance by group using the same
Ledoit-Wolf Σ `_decompose` fits: variance contributed by the group's
weights = $w_g' \Sigma w_g + w_g' \Sigma w_{-g}$ (own plus cross), as a
share of $w'\Sigma w$.

**2. The spread chart.** Define the muni-vs-taxable discount spread:
$$S_t = \overline{d}_{muni,t} - \overline{d}_{taxable,t}$$
(equal-weight mean discount of the muni names minus the mean of the
others, on the eligible set each day). Plot five years of $S_t$ with its
own 252-day rolling mean and ±1/±2 sd bands (same construction as the
per-name z, so the trader reads it the same way), and its current z.
Overlay a marker: "book is SHORT this spread; PC2 loading = 92.5%; net
muni weight −74.9%". Add the same chart for multi-minus-rest as a second,
smaller panel.

**3. A one-line reading, computed not written:** "Muni-vs-taxable spread
is at z = +x.x against its own history; the book is short it. Historical
net Sharpe of the live policy by spread-z quintile: … (from a one-off
calculation cached in `results/cef/spread_quintiles.json`)". This is the
dispersion-quintile table of `docs/PLAN.md` §3.1 applied to the group
spread instead of total dispersion. **It is a mirror, not a rule**: the
repo measured that sizing on dislocation lowers Sharpe, and this panel
must say so in its footer.

**4. History sparkline:** BR_eff and PC2 share per session from
`_br_history`, labelled ledger-derived.

### Server

`/api/groups`: the table, the spread series, the quintile reading, the
history. Groups come from the universe csv; a name with no group raises
(it cannot be "other"). Σ from the cached `_decompose` fit. The spread is
computed on the cached discount panel with eligibility applied per date
(a group with fewer than 2 eligible names on a date has no mean that
day; leave the gap).

### Acceptance

- The group table's gross and net sum to the book's gross and net shown
  in the masthead within rounding.
- The variance shares across groups plus the cross terms sum to 100%.
- The spread's current z on the chart matches the one-line reading.
- The footer carries the sentence: "Sizing on this level was measured and
  lowers Sharpe (PLAN §3.1). This panel is a mirror."

### Do not

- Do not turn the reading into a trading rule or a sizing input.
- Do not label PC2 "muni" by hard-coding names; the classifier in
  `_decompose` decides, and the group membership comes from the csv.
