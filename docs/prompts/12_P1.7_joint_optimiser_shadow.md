### The objective and what falls out of it

$$\max_w\; w'\alpha - \tfrac{\lambda}{2} w'\Sigma w - c\,\lVert w - w_{prev}\rVert_1
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \mathbf 1'w = 0$$

The $\ell_1$ term produces a no-trade region as a **consequence**: a name is
left alone unless its risk-adjusted marginal alpha, $|\alpha_i - \lambda
(\Sigma w)_i - \nu|$, clears $c$. The region is Σ-aware, so a muni is not
traded merely because its own z moved if the rest of the muni block
already carries the exposure. That is why it beats the scalar band at
matched turnover and why composing Σ⁻¹α with a scalar band does not.

### Inputs per session (all with stated alignment)

- $z_i$: the live signal (252d moments, shift 1, clip ±4).
- $\sigma_i$: `ret.rolling(252).std().shift(1)`. Grinold alpha $\alpha_i =
  \sigma_i \cdot (-(z_i - \bar z))$.
- $\Sigma$: Ledoit-Wolf on the trailing 252 complete-case returns ending
  the day before, refit on the first session of each month and cached;
  `_cov_window`'s rule for names with too few observations, and it
  **raises** if a held name cannot be estimated.
- Eligibility: ADV screen as the sleeve; ineligible names are excluded
  from the solve and emitted as FLAT targets (closed), never left as free
  variables.
- $w_{prev}$: the held weights from `market_state.holdings` at the sizing
  close over sleeve NAV, the same self-correcting reference the band uses.
  A missed session leaves $w_{prev}$ further from the optimum and the next
  solve closes it; no stored target.
- $\lambda$: `lambda_for_vol` so that the $c = 0$ solution's ex-ante vol
  hits the 6% target (bounded by the same 0.2–2.5 range the scalar has,
  expressed as a gross bound; document the mapping).
- $c$: `c_model` in return units. Default 25.2bp expressed as the
  objective's units (see the script). P5.2 derives it; until then use the
  turnover-matched value and record that it is matched, not derived.
- Solve: `solve(alpha, Sigma, lam, c, w_prev)`. `SolverError` propagates:
  a session where the shadow cannot certify is logged as "no solve", never
  a fallback to the band.

Output: `PositionTarget` per name; names with `d_i == 0` exactly are
HOLDs and are emitted **qty-expressed** with the held quantity; traded
names weight-expressed; closed names FLAT.
