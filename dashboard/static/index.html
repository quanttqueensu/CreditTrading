Cheap vs rich

Discount z-score against each fund's own 252-day history: how unusually wide today's discount to NAV is for that specific fund. The book is cross-sectional and dollar-neutral, so cheap and rich are judged against today's mean z across the universe — not against zero. A fund can sit at a wide discount in absolute terms and still be sold, because every other fund is wider.
