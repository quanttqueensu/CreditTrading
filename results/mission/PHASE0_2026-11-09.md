The first report of the mission (`docs/MISSION.md` §10, Phase 0). Three parts: why the book
has too few bets and what is most likely to fix it; which data is current; and what the

## 1. Diagnosis: why the book has too few bets

**The short version.** The desk has one signal, on one kind of instrument, traded once a day,
and it spent its research effort proving ideas wrong at a bar almost nothing passes. The
fix is more unrelated mechanisms on much larger universes, and most of the starting
material is already in the repository.

### What the book is today

- **Two paper sleeves, one signal.** The core (13 credit CEFs) and C4 (74 funds in five more
  CEF families) both trade the discount z-score. C4-LEVEL adds the discount level inside C4
  [S: `docs/RESEARCH_STATE.md`, ACTIVE]. Prod is the VM, armed, at `release-20261104-1`; the
  core account's equity was $98,838.46 at the 2026-11-09 close against $100,000 funded
  [V: `python3 -m ops.orient`].
- **The core's signal is weak where it trades.** The deployed 13-name IC is −0.033 (t −1.68)
  on 523 dates from 2013-06, significant in no single era, with half the summed IC on
  December and January dates. Fit period to 2022-12-30 [S: `docs/SYSTEM.md` §2, from
  `results/cef/W4_ARTIFACTS_2026-10-11.md`].
- **Its names are one bet.** Effective breadth was 1.17 on the live weights and 2.24
  historically, because most of the variance is municipal against taxable funds. Measured
  2026-09-07 on the 17-name book, not re-measured since [S: `docs/BRIEF.md` §1; stale].
- **It is small.** The 13 names ran from $1.26m to $31.5m of daily dollar volume on CRSP 2024
  [S: `docs/BRIEF.md` §3]. A book at 1.8× gross on $10M puts about $1.4M in an average name,
  which is more than a day's volume in the thinnest ones. The core cannot be the fund at the
  target size. C4's 74 names help, and are the same trade.

### Why the count of bets stayed low

1. **One mechanism.** Every live sleeve is retail-driven discount reversion in closed-end
   funds. In `IR ≈ IC × √breadth`, the desk worked on IC, which its own notes call the least
   productive place to work, and never added a second mechanism.
2. **The gates removed the inventory.** Under the old rules an idea needed a pre-registration,
   a deflated-Sharpe bar that rose with every test (2.83 at the CEF counter's 55), net
   profit at 15bp of assumed cost, and no factor exposure. `results/IDEA_LEDGER.md` lists 13
   ideas with a measured positive gross effect that one of those gates stopped. The clearest are
   month-end Treasury demand (gross Sharpe 1.04, t 4.94), the ex-date drift (t −14.9 across
   the CEF families), the opening-auction fill (+293bp a year on the book's own trades), NAV
   tenders (+1.08% an event) and the PCEF reconstitution [S: `docs/RESEARCH_STATE.md`].
   The old ledger itself says a Sharpe of 0.3 to 0.6 is "inventory, not failure", and then
   kept none.
3. **Small universes by design.** Trend was tested on 9 and 14 ETFs, pairs on 22, the core on
   13. A cross-sectional signal on 13 names cannot be distinguished from noise in a
   reasonable time, which is the power failure the old statistician kept reporting.
4. **One horizon and one clock.** Everything decides in the evening and fills at the next
   close. Nothing trades an open, an intraday window, a dated event or a monthly cycle.
5. **Effort went to operations.** Since 2026-09-28 the work has been the Alpaca migration, the
   VM, the gates and the data defects. That bought a reliable runner. It is the asset the
   next sleeves stand on.
6. **Each new sleeve needed a prod release and its own account.** C4 took from 2026-10-14
   (approved) to 2026-11-04 (first session) to reach paper. The pipeline in the mission needs
   that to be a day.

### What is most likely to fix it, in order

1. **A paper and shadow runner outside the release path** (`docs/MISSION.md` §8.1). Without
   it nothing below reaches paper quickly. A shadow book needs no account at all: it writes
   target positions each day and is marked on official closes. This is Phase 1's first
   build, and it needs the team lead's one-time approval of the design.
2. **Put the gross-positive inventory on shadow first.** Month-end Treasury, the ex-date
   drift, the opening-auction fill and the PCEF reconstitution all have code and fit-period
   results: the opening-auction and ex-date scripts on `main`, month-end on branch
   `trial-xasset`, the PCEF book on branch `sleeve-pcef` [V: `git ls-tree`, 2026-11-09]. They are the cheapest independent bets
   available, and two of them (ex-date, month-end) are close to uncorrelated with the
   discount signal by construction.
3. **Wide cross-sections in liquid names.** The R2 mirror holds 1,146 tables in 81 schemas:
   CRSP, Compustat, IBES, Thomson insiders and 13F, SDC deals. It is one snapshot written
   2026-06-26 to 07-03 and not refreshed since [S: `docs/DATA.md` §3]. Single-name equity signals on a
   few thousand stocks (post-earnings drift, short-term reversal, insider clusters, short
   interest, index events) give hundreds of bets a month and room for $10M. The SSX-1
   census already counted eight event families and tested none.
4. **Futures for premia and flows.** Trend, carry and the month-end and auction-cycle effects
   need 30 to 50 liquid futures, not 14 ETFs. Scope and alpha-only no longer stand in the
   way. The local futures panel ends 2026-04-08 and needs a free continuous-contract build.
5. **A shared market-state layer.** The macro filters did nothing for the core
   [S: `results/cef/screens_2026-10-26/r1_macro_state/NOTE.md`], so state is unlikely to
   rescue a weak signal. Its use is sizing the whole book and switching conditional sleeves
   such as the fallen-angel flow, which fires only in stress.
6. **Then the combined model.** `results/cef/PORTFOLIO_FRAMEWORK_2026-10-15.md` and the dark
   multi-sleeve combiner in `quantt/` are the starting point.

### What I am unsure of

- **Whether the gross effects survive real trading.** Several of the 13 were stopped on cost
  for a reason: month-end Treasury turns over 58 times a year. The mission's answer is to
  measure that on paper. Alpaca paper fills at the quote and charges no borrow, so paper will
  flatter anything short-heavy or auction-dependent; the go-live proposal has to carry that.
- **Whether a rising bar was hiding real flukes.** With the bar gone, the time split and the
  paper record are the only defence. I will treat a result that holds in one half only as
  not found.
- **The core itself.** Its account is below its funding after six weeks, which says little
  on its own, and its deployed IC is not significant. Phase 3 should ask plainly whether it earns its place in the combined
  book. I have not measured that.
