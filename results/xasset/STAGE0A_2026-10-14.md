`python3 scripts/rv/panel_identity.py data/etf_daily.parquet results/xasset/_cache_xasset/etf_daily_xasset.parquet`,
2026-10-14, compared through the old panel's last date, 2026-10-09 (68,058

|---|---:|---|

| `ret_total`, CRSP leg (to 2024-12-31) | **0** | — |
| `ret_total`, Alpaca leg (2026-07-21 → 2026-10-09) | **0** | — |
| `ret_total`, yfinance leg (2025-01-02 → 2026-07-20) | **3,210 of 5,018** | max 0.0074bp |

- **Every differing cell is in the yfinance leg's `ret_total`,** which is
  Yahoo Adj Close `pct_change`. Cut through 2024-12-31 the two files are
  byte-identical, and with `ret_total` excluded they are byte-identical
  through 2026-10-09.
- **No rebuild can remove the difference.** A second `--xasset` build, made
  minutes after the first, differs from the first in the same way: 6,112
  yfinance-leg `ret_total` cells, max 0.0075bp, and nothing else. REFERENCES §8
  (2026-10-11) had already recorded that "no series derived from Yahoo Adj
  Close can be reproduced bitwise, by any fetcher".
- **What the difference touches.** It lies wholly inside the holdout
  (2023-01-03 → 2026-09-30). It is four orders of magnitude below the build's
  own 5bp splice gate. The fit stages read only the CRSP leg, which is
  identical.

**The default build path is unchanged by the DG1 code.** Run without
`--xasset` to a scratch path (`results/xasset/_cache_xasset/`), the build
writes the same 13 tickers and 8 columns. Against `data/etf_daily.parquet` it
differs only in the same yfinance-leg `ret_total` cells (3,067), and it is
byte-identical through 2026-10-09 with `ret_total` excluded. `data/` was not
written.

**The question for the team lead.** DG1 says "rebuild unchanged in every
existing column" and names a tool whose verdict is byte identity. That cannot
hold for the yfinance leg's `ret_total`, in any rebuild. Does DG1 clear on the
measured result:

- except vendor re-basing of Adj Close in the 2025-01-02 → 2026-07-20
  `ret_total`, at most 0.0075bp?

Or does it need something else? The run does not answer this itself (rule 5).

session. None fits the stub-print pattern of the build's own scan (\|r\| > 8%
on fewer than 50,000 shares):

- **8 are crisis days on at least 1.05× their 63-session median volume:**
  HYG 2008-09-17 and 2020-03-12; LQD 2008-09-29 and 2020-03-12; PFF
  2008-09-17; SPY 2020-03-12; BKLN 2020-03-31; MUB 2010-12-17.

  fund, which a zero MAD turns into an infinite z.
