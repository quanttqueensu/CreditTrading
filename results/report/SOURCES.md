# Sources for the research report: literature and primary sources

**A running list, not a literature review.** Each row names a source a later report on this desk
could cite, the claim it would support, and its status. Started 2026-11-03 for the evidence pack
(`results/report/README.md`); add rows as sources are found. Reproducer for the pack's figures:
`python3 -m scripts.report.build_pack` (this file has no figures of its own).

Status, two kinds only:
- **FETCHED <date>: <what was read>**: the page or PDF was loaded and read in that session. Every
  FETCHED row is also a `[V]` row in `docs/REFERENCES.md` (§12bo for the 2026-11-03 batch), which
  stays the owner of the claim's verification status.
- **TO VERIFY**: not fetched (or the fetch failed, and the row says what was tried). Do not quote a
  TO VERIFY source's numbers; fetch it first (CLAUDE.md, data rules).

How the 2026-11-03 batch was read (the literature seat's own note): SR 11-7, SR 26-2 and the
Frazzini–Israel–Moskowitz draft were downloaded and read as extracted text; every other FETCHED row was
read through a web-fetch tool that relays excerpts of at most 125 characters, so its quotes are
excerpts, and anything paraphrased is marked as a paraphrase. Rows that point at an existing
`docs/REFERENCES.md` section were not re-fetched and are TO VERIFY at their recorded status.
