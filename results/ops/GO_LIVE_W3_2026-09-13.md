## Not done, and why

- **The gateway listens on all interfaces** with `AcceptIncomingConnectionAction=accept`
  (`~/ibc/config.ini`). Set it to `reject` and tick "localhost only" in Gateway.
- **Option legs are not covered by the pending-order guard** (matched on the OCC
  localSymbol). Nothing trades options; gamma/G5 must extend it first.
- **After-hours executions** are dated UTC while capture coverage is judged in
  ET — latent for the MOC-only cef book, open for any after-hours book.
- **Tracked `_pre_epoch_*` ledger directories were not archived**: moving a
  tracked path through a promotion deletes prod's copy.
- **launch_job still files `armed=True` on rc 5/6** (a guarded stand-down);
  `session_plan` now excludes by status, but the beat itself still says armed.
