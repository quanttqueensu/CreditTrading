**Reading.** Filling at the open does earn the session: +293bp/yr on WIDE's
holdings path. But **the same open fill applied to the trade decided a day
earlier earns at least as much (+351bp/yr)** over the same session. The gain
therefore does not need the decision to be fresh. It is a session drift on
the signal's trades, whichever day they were decided, and not the arrival of
*t*'s NAV that C5's mechanism (§2) requires.

The pre-registration named the false-kill risk in advance (§9: "the signal
decays slowly and the placebo will not be near zero"). It committed the rule
anyway, and the rule is not softened here.

The gain also fails the other two decision tests:
- **Risk premium (D4):** it loads on MUB's intraday return.
- **The bar:** it is fat-tailed. Kurtosis is 290, March 2020 alone holds
  session returns of up to 21%, and `_dsr` is 0.61.

## 1. What halted it

The pre-registration lists three examples of a defect outside the declared
classes, any one of which halts. The run tested each one on the 21,628 panel
name-days of the decision window, 2016-01-04 → 2022-12-30:

| §4.5 example | found |
|---|---|
| (a) an opening print at an impossible price | 0 |
| (b) a session return of 50% or more (the harness's filter) | 0. The largest was 21.0% (JFR, 2020-03-18), where the print agrees with both consolidated opens |
| (c) **a panel close that disagrees with both CRSP `prc` and the NYSE closing print** | **1, at a robust reading; 22 on the stored floats** (below) |

**The one that halts under any reading: MHD, 2017-06-21.** The panel close
(yfinance) is **3.00c above** the NYSE closing trade (condition `6`), and off
CRSP `prc` as well; CRSP and the NYSE print agree with each other. *(Corrected
2026-10-15: the per-date CRSP difference this sentence first gave is no longer
printed, because beside the public close it recovers CRSP's licensed price.)* It falls on a **material
WIDE trade name-day**: a trade decided on 2017-06-20 that C5 would fill at that
day's open and whose session return reads this close. It is not on a trade of
the deployed set.

**The other 21 are a tolerance question.** Each is **exactly half a cent** off
CRSP `prc`. The panel stores prices as float32 (10.89 is held as
10.890000343), so an exact half cent reads as just over the declared $0.005.
Eight of the 21 fall on WIDE material trades, and none on the deployed set's.

| reading of "disagrees" (> $0.005) | all name-days | WIDE material trades | deployed material trades |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| strict, on the stored float32 values | 22 | 9 | 0 |
| robust: an exact half cent is agreement (> $0.005 + 1e-6) | **1** | **1** | 0 |

The pre-registration states no tolerance for "disagrees". The run applied
§4.2's declared $0.005 and printed both readings. **The halt fires under
both.**
