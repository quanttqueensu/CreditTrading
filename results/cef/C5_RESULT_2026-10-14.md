## 1. What halted it

The pre-registration lists three examples of a defect outside the declared
classes, any one of which halts. The run tested each one on the 21,628 panel
name-days of the decision window, 2016-01-04 → 2022-12-30:

| §4.5 example | found |
|---|---|
| (a) an opening print at an impossible price | 0 |
| (b) a session return of 50% or more (the harness's filter) | 0. The largest was 21.0% (JFR, 2020-03-18), where the print agrees with both consolidated opens |
| (c) **a panel close that disagrees with both CRSP `prc` and the NYSE closing print** | **1, at a robust reading; 22 on the stored floats** (below) |

**The one that halts under any reading: MHD, 2017-06-21.** The panel close
(yfinance) is **3.00c above both** CRSP `prc` and the NYSE closing trade
(condition `6`), and those two agree with each other. It falls on a **material
WIDE trade name-day**: a trade decided on 2017-06-20 that C5 would fill at that
day's open and whose session return reads this close. It is not on a trade of
the deployed set.

**The other 21 are a tolerance question.** Each is **exactly half a cent** off
CRSP `prc`. The panel stores prices as float32 (10.89 is held as
10.890000343), so an exact half cent reads as just over the declared $0.005.
Eight of the 21 fall on WIDE material trades, and none on the deployed set's.

| reading of "disagrees" (> $0.005) | all name-days | WIDE material trades | deployed material trades |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| strict, on the stored float32 values | 22 | 9 | 0 |
| robust: an exact half cent is agreement (> $0.005 + 1e-6) | **1** | **1** | 0 |

The pre-registration states no tolerance for "disagrees". The run applied
§4.2's declared $0.005 and printed both readings. **The halt fires under
both.**

## 3. Questions for the team lead

1. **The MHD 2017-06-21 close.** §4.5 says a vendor defect "is fixed in its
   fetcher with a dated note, and Stage 0 re-runs". The fetcher writes
   `data/cef/cef_prices.parquet`, which the live sleeve decides on
   (landmine 9), and the deployed baselines are priced on the same close. The
   options, none taken here:
   - repair the row through the fetcher, then re-run Stage 0;
   - rule that such a name-day is a no-cross name-day for the gain (a new
     declared class, with a halt threshold). No return of C5 has been seen,
     but the count has;
   - another ruling.
2. **The tolerance for "disagrees" in §4.5(c).** Should an exact half cent
   on float32-stored prices count as agreement? That is the "robust" reading
   (1 name-day) against the "strict" one (22). Either way the halt in item 1
   stands.
3. **Decisions a re-run will need that the document does not make.** They are
   flagged now so that a second run does not stop on each in turn. None was
   resolved here.
   - **(a) The signed-dislocation cost row (§6).** It sets the D2-open
     label. Is its mean per material trade name-day, like `s_open`'s mean per
     name-day, or weighted by |ΔH| (per unit of turnover)?
   - **(b) R-D4's two cross-sectional legs (§10).** Is the 13 names' session
     return the trial's own `O` print → panel close, or CRSP `openprc` →
     `prc`, as the ETFs use? Is the ETFs' lagged close-to-close return CRSP
     `ret` (total) or the `prc` change?
   - **(c) The shuffled-trade placebo (§9, 2c).** 1,762 sessions are 83
     blocks of 21 plus one of 19. Is the short block permuted as a block?
   - **(d) The stale-open stratum (§9, 2a).** Where Cₛ₋₁ is missing, is the
     name-day in the "moved" stratum or out of it?
   - **(e) R-CLOSE (§11).** It names RUNNER's "`M` on `N`" rule, but NYSE
     published no `M` before 2020-11-04. Is the rule `M` else `6` (the EXEC
     note's)? And is a name-day with `M` but no `6` (no round-lot cross) a
     no-cross name-day?
   - **(f) The capacity-bound label (§9).** How is it read if the quote
     sample's gain is ≤ 0?
4. **DG-C5-7.** The holdout can be opened only after the panels in the tree
   that runs it reach 2026-09-30 and 2026-09-29. This worktree reads `data/`
   read-only, so that needs a panel refresh first.
