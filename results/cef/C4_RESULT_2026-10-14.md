## 4. What was not done, and what a ruling unlocks

**Not computed.** Stage 0's remaining items have not been run: C4's turnover,
the share of name-days on which the band binds, the cap-binding share, and
the realised volatility against the target. All of them need C4's holdings
path. Its vol scalar reads the trailing returns of C4's own weights, and the
realised volatility is a moment of C4's P&L. The run's rule is that the data
gates halt before any return is computed, so these wait for the gate. Stages
1–9 are not written: `scripts/cef/c4_families.py` holds Stage 0 only.

**After the ruling:**

1. Encode the ruling in `stage0` and re-run it until it is clear.
2. Run Stage 0's diagnostics.
3. Run Stages 1–6 and commit the fit verdict.
4. Then, and only then, open the holdout once (Stage 8).
