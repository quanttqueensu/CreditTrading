**The ruling (team lead, 2026-10-14; PREREG amendment 2).**

## 4b. The review of `3be92cd` (2026-10-15): BTX's CRSP security and the identity check

**BTX was on the wrong CRSP security.**

- The families map (`data/cef_families/crsp_permno_map.csv`) tied BTX to
  permno 76963: NTN Buzztime, then Brooklyn Immunotherapeutics, then Eterna
  Therapeutics, share code 11, a biotech that used the ticker BTX in 2021–22.
- yfinance's BTX history is **BIGZ's**: BlackRock Innovation & Growth Term
  Trust, renamed BTX on 2025-02-18 (REFERENCES §12k).
- **CRSP stocknames gives permno 20674**: ticker BIGZ, "BLACKROCK INNOVATION
  & GROWTH TR", share code 44, NYSE, 2021-03-26 → 2024-12-31.
- **Verified:** its closing print equals yfinance's BTX close within $0.005
  on 947 of 948 days (99.89%). The one miss is the IPO day ($20.00 against
  $20.02). The volume ratio is 1.000.
- **The override is in code** (`stage_crsp_cef_families.PERMNO_OVERRIDES`,
  checked by `verify_override` every time it is staged). It applies only to
  the gitignored C4 return cache; `data/` is not written.
- BTX's Stage-0 flags fell from Q6 940 and Q5 45 to 1 and 1.

**The identity check** (`c4_families.crsp_identity`, run by `crsp_pivot` and
by Stage 0 per window). For every fund it requires:

- every row's CRSP share code is a CEF's (14 or 44);
- the daily price move agrees, |yfinance close change − CRSP `retx`| ≤ 10bp,
  on at least 99% of common days. This is the reviewer's metric.

Its results:

| window | the 74 | the 105 (R-S) |
|---|---|---|
| **fit, 2005–2022** | **all pass**; lowest GGN 0.9902; BTX/BIGZ 0.9978 | all pass |
| **holdout CRSP leg, 2023–24** | **FAIL: GCV 0.908, GGN 0.914, GLO 0.966, GLV 0.984** | the same, plus GNT and IGD |

**The holdout-leg failures are not wrong securities.**

- They pass the fit window, and their share codes are CEF codes.
- CRSP prints half-cents on these sub-$5 funds (GGN 3.795) where yfinance
  rounds to the cent (3.80). That is 13bp on a $3.80 fund, and it occurs
  mostly in 2023–24.
- Even so, the check fails as specified, and it halts the holdout's CRSP leg
  until the team lead rules.

**Under the team lead's tolerance of 2026-10-15** (a move agrees within
max(10bp, $0.005/price)):

- **fit window:** all 74 pass; the lowest is PEO at 0.9923;
- **holdout CRSP leg:** **GCV 0.956, GGN 0.950, GLO 0.980 and GLV 0.986
  still fail**, so the holdout halts until the team lead rules.

**Why half a cent is not enough:**

- Every yfinance close of these four funds is within exactly $0.005 of CRSP's
  print, a maximum of $0.0050: they are the right securities.
- A half-cent rounding at t−1 moves the measured return by
  0.005 · p_t / p_{t−1}², which sits at or just beyond 0.005 / p_{t−1}.
- At max(10bp, **$0.01**/price) all four pass; the lowest is GGN at 0.998.

**Two options, neither chosen:** widen the tolerance to $0.01/price, or
exclude the four funds as amendment 3 excludes the distribution failures.
**Ruled 2026-10-16 (amendment 4): widened to $0.01/price** (§4c).

**A level check is reported beside it, never deciding:** the close within
$0.005, after undoing yfinance's split factors.

- In the fit window it fails **GLV** (19% of days), because yfinance's GLV
  close is a constant 0.999 × CRSP's print through 2019, a 0.1% re-basing.
- It also fails GLU at 98.9%.
- The move metric is the one encoded, because a constant re-basing is not a
  different security.

**The calendar.** `crsp_pivot` now asserts that every CRSP trading day in its
