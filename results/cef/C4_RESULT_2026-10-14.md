## 4. Why the holdout is not open: a question for the team lead

The holdout's primary return from 2025-01-02 is the declared yfinance formula:
(1 + r_px)·k − 1 + D/P(t−1), with yfinance's own dividends. The source note is
in REFERENCES §12h.

Stage 0 cross-checked that leg against Alpaca's corporate actions. This check
is an addition to the screen, needed for ruling 2.

**The result:** 71 of 1,294 ex-dates on 11 of the 74 funds disagree (beyond
$0.001, or on one side only). The list is in
`results/cef/c4/c4_yf_leg_distribution_disagreements_2026-10-14.csv`:

| class | funds (ex-dates) | yfinance against Alpaca |
|---|---|---|
| a split applied to dividends but not to the raw close | **IGR (20)** | ×3.000: 0.18 against 0.06 on every ex-date before the 2026-09-08 1:3 reverse split. Each one books a phantom yield of about 2.6% on IGR |
| a constant ~2% scaling | UTF (9; yfinance records a 1.017 "split" on 2025-09-22), BUI (14), EMO (8), NXG (7), SCD (5) | ×0.976–0.983 |
| year-end distributions short in yfinance | **TY (2)**, ADX (1), PEO (1) | TY 2025-12-12: 0.271 against 2.3154, while TY's NAV fell 2.59 that day. TY 2025-06-16: 0.29 against 1.052 |
| one side only | BTX (1), IGA (2, ex-dates one session apart), NXG (1) | — |

Closes agree on 32,144 of 32,337 fund-days within $0.005.

**The fit is unaffected:**

- its returns are CRSP;
- the signal reads prices and NAVs, never a distribution;
- the R-yf row and the NAV catch-up diagnostic use yfinance dividends, and are
  labelled as including the defect.

**The pre-registration** (§4.5) says "a flag found to be a vendor defect is
fixed in the fetcher with a dated note before any P&L". It does not say how,
and this run cannot write `data/cef_families/`. The options are below; **none
is chosen**.

| option | what it does |
|---|---|
| (a) | Use Alpaca's distributions for the whole yfinance leg (all 74 funds) |
| (b) | Un-adjust yfinance's dividends by yfinance's own later split factors. This reproduces Alpaca to the cent for IGR and UTF. It leaves BUI, EMO, NXG, SCD, TY, ADX, PEO and the one-sided rows as they are |
| (c) | Correct only the confirmed internal inconsistency (IGR, by (b)) and keep the declared source elsewhere |
| (d) | Keep the declared source unchanged and report the holdout with and without the defect |

**Reading of the ordering rule.** This run read §4.5's "before any P&L" as
"before any P&L the repair can touch", and therefore ran the fit:

- no repair of the 2025–26 distributions can change a fit number;
- the amendment's own rule for a gap in the holdout source is that "the
  holdout halts".

If the team lead reads §4.5 more strictly, the fit verdict above was seen
before the repair was ruled. It is recorded here so that the reading is
visible, not inherited.
