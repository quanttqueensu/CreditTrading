# ssx_risk: subtract-only risk flags for the live CEF discount book (iteration 1), 2026-10-19

**Seat:** risk-flag designer. **Zero trials. No counter moved.** I computed no return, P&L, CAR, drift or Sharpe for any fund or event window. The measurements are holdings weights, the funds' own bond marks (read as staleness statistics), event counts, flags and terms.

**Data limits.**
- N-PORT holdings are read only for report dates up to 2022-12-31. No 2023+ market data was read.
- Event counts run to 2025.
- The Alpaca flags are today's (read 2026-10-19T19:56Z).

**Live requests.**
- EDGAR: 268 of my 800, all at `min_interval=1.5`:
  - 1 probe;
  - 236 N-PORT documents;
  - 3 N-CSRs;
  - 28 BDC submissions files.
- Alpaca: 2 read-only `GET /v2/assets`.

**Labels.** [V] = fetched or produced by the named script today. [S] = sourced, not re-read. [U] = unverified.

**E·S·F, 0–3 each:**
- **E (evidence)**: does the flag remove a real loss?
- **S (scale)**: how often does it bind on the deployed 13?
- **F (feasibility)**: is there a lawful, buildable live feed?

## B1. Muni credit events mapped to muni CEF holdings

- **Mechanism; who loses.**
  - An EMMA event hits a held bond: a Rule 15c2-12 notice such as a P&I delinquency, a reserve draw or a rating change.
  - The listed price moves, but the matrix-priced NAV keeps the mark.
  - The z-score then buys the "cheap" fund. The loser is the book.
- **Clock.**
  - Notice due "in a timely manner not in excess of ten business days after the occurrence" (Rule 15c2-12(b)(5)(i)(C)) [V].
  - Holdings: N-PORT, public 60 days after the fiscal quarter, one snapshot per quarter.
- **Rule shape.** No new long, or no grown long, on a fund with event-affected weight ≥ w. It never adds an order.
- **Measured** (`ssx_risk_b1_muni.py`; N-PORT report dates 2019-10-31 → 2022-11-30; 7 funds, 84 fund-quarters):

| quantity | MQY+MHD (deployed) | NAD NEA NVG NZF NAN | source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| new default/arrears flags, bp of NAV per fund-quarter: median / mean / p90 | **0 / 0 / 0** (24 fund-quarters, none flagged) | 0.85 / 12.4 / 36.1 (53% of fund-quarters have any) | [V] |
| stock flagged in default, median bp | 0 | 42.3 | [V] |
| non-IG share of CS01 (the fund's own split), median | MQY 13.8%, MHD 15.9% | NVG 25.7%, NZF 27.4%, NAD 9.7%, NEA 8.6%, NAN 15.3% | [V] |
| below-IG + not-rated share of total investments, per N-CSR | MQY 13.5%, MHD 15.0% (2022-07-31) | NVG 30.2% (2022-04-30) | [V] |
| idiosyncratic mark drop ≥10pt beyond the maturity×coupon bucket median, median bp | 16.7 (p90 211) | 11.3 | [V] |

- **(a) Feed and terms.**
  - EMMA terms (last updated 2026-01-01) [V]: no "data mining, crawling, 'scraping', robot or similar automated or data gathering or extraction method … or otherwise systematically download or store Content". They also say "Certain data … is available by subscription, for a fee".
  - MSRB Continuing Disclosure feed: **$45,000/yr**. Historical product: **$22,500 per 12 months, plus $2,000** for first-time buyers [V]. Both are paid.
  - EMMA displays **current ratings only**, "does not display historical ratings information" [V].
- **(b) Free counts.**
  - **MSRB Fact Books, documents per year 2019–24** [V; the overlapping years in five books agree]:
    - P&I delinquency: 401, 350, 361, 319, 239, 194;
    - unscheduled reserve draw: 99, 140, 128, 82, 97, 123;
    - non-payment default: 98, 97, 54, 53, 81, 76;
    - bankruptcy/insolvency/receivership: 81, 52, 129, 58, 64, 92.
  - **Chapter 9 cases** (FJC file via R2, a lower bound): **129** in 2008–25, median **5/yr**, peak 21 in 2012, 2 in 2025 [V].
- **(d) Do the funds' own marks lag?** Descriptive only.
  - Default-flagged holdings keep an exactly unchanged quarterly mark **23.3%** of the time (1,474 pairs). Unflagged holdings: **0.2%** (62,944 pairs) [V].
  - Of 175 new flags (84 CUSIPs), **73%** landed on bonds already marked below 90. Only **5%** came with a drop of 5 points or more. **The N-PORT flag lags the mark**, so it cannot be the early warning.
  - Same CUSIP, same date, BlackRock against Nuveen (3,053 pairs): median gap **0.46pt**, p90 1.86, and 27% at 1pt or more. Within one sponsor the marks are identical: Nuveen differs by 0.01pt or more in 0 of 10,650 pairs, BlackRock in 1.6% of 2,350 [V].
- **Two methods disagree; I picked neither.** The mark-drop proxy clears 10bp at the median. Its median by year is 2020 22.6bp, **2021 0.0bp** and 2022 24.0bp, and its biggest values are the market-wide sell-off quarters: 2020-04, MQY 787bp and MHD 1,572bp. So it measures market moves, not credit events, and it is not a live feed. **The verdict rests on the event measure.**
- **Graveyard.**
  - `single-fund-PD`: the stale-mark well, wet here.
  - `S1-as-specified`: marks are uniform within a sponsor.
  - `nport-trace-nav`.
- **Counter.** 0 to measure; CEF +1 to adopt.
- **Kill: both legs fail.**

## B2. Disasters and state fiscal shocks against single-state muni CEFs

- **Mechanism.** A state disaster moves the listed price before the NAV re-marks. The loser is the book.
- **Clock and source.** OpenFEMA's declaration date (DR = major disaster).
- **Rule shape.** No new long, or no grown long, on single-state funds of the affected state for N sessions.
- **Measured** (`ssx_risk_b2_disasters.py`):
  - **1,161** major-disaster declarations, 2008–2025.
  - **Single-state muni CEFs** on EDGAR: **143** in 2008 → **54** in 2025. The 2025 figure includes 18 unlisted Puerto Rico resident funds.
  - **Independent (state, disaster) pairs: median 17 a year.** 17 of the 18 years fall below 30; the maximum is 37, in 2011. 2021–25 median: 16. State-month median: 15.5.
  - **Alpaca today:** 52 single-state muni symbols, of which 22 are CEFs and 7 are shortable and ETB: NAZ, MUC, NCA, MYN, NNY, VTN, NPV.
  - The 44-name universe holds only **NAN**, which is not shortable. **The deployed 13 hold none.**
  - State budget-crisis episodes were **not dated or sourced** (a gap).
- **Terms.** OpenFEMA (updated 2025-01-22): data "used solely for statistical research or as a reporting record". **Whether a live trading flag fits that clause is [U]; the team lead decides.** Required notice: "This product uses the Federal Emergency Management Agency's OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA."
- **Graveyard.** CA9 and MA1 (calendar-staleness flags, queued).
- **Counter.** 0 to measure; CEF +1 to adopt.
- **Kill: KILL.**

## B4. Taxable downgrades and defaults mapped through N-PORT

- **Measured** (`ssx_risk_b4_downgrades.py`):
  - 11 deployed taxable funds, events 2019-12 → 2022-12, 143 fund-quarters.
  - **4,341** issuer-downgrade days: 336 IG→HY and 1,532 severe (into CCC+ or worse, or 3+ notches).
  - Panel ends: Moody's 2023-12-15, Fitch 2025-06-30. **S&P is absent.**

|---|---:|---:|

| share of fund-quarters at 10bp or more | 89.5% | 93.7% |
| severe-only median bp (share of fund-quarters with any) | 75 (83%) | 85 (86%) |
| IG→HY median bp (share with any) | 0 (38%) | 0 (43%) |
| new N-PORT default flags: median / mean bp (share with any) | 0 / 29.6 (44%) | — |

- **Rating-panel coverage of credit weight: median 50.3%.**
  - HYT 92%, JFR 85%, BIT 58%, AWF 58%.
  - **PIMCO 26–51%** (PDI 26%) and DSL 45%. Structured credit and MBS fall outside the panel's categories.
- **How it differs from `nport-trace-nav`.**
  - That idea needed a print for every bond, every day. TRACE gave 17–30%, selected on news.
  - A flag needs only "an event touched weight w". Coverage comes from the ratings, not from trading.
  - Quarterly holdings are no obstacle here: the point-in-time and as-of medians are 267 and 269bp, because issuer exposure persists across quarters.
- **How it does not differ.**
  - The 17g-7 histories are published "within twelve months" for issuer-paid ratings and 24 months for others (Rule 17g-7(b)(4)) [V]. **So they are research history, not a live feed.** The terms of a same-day agency press-release feed are [U].
  - **The flag would bind in about 90% of fund-quarters.** As specified it is not selective. Iteration 2 must measure how much of the band's turnover is decided on flagged name-days, on the severe definition.
- **Graveyard.** `nport-trace-nav` (D7), `S3-wrapper` (D4) and CR3.
- **Counter.** 0 to measure; CEF +1 to adopt.
- **Verdict.** **Passes iteration 1's census gate, with coverage on the line.** It is not yet a flag.

## What I could not establish

- A lawful **live** feed for muni events or agency downgrades.
  - EMMA bans automated collection.
  - The agency press-release terms were not read.
- Whether OpenFEMA's purpose clause allows a trading flag.
- State budget-crisis episodes, dated and sourced.
- Whether a single-copy curl save of five MSRB PDFs is within "view, print and make copies". The script does not download for that reason.
- Why the PIMCO funds flag 4–26% of NAV in default (average bp per snapshot: PDO 3.9%, PFN 8.9%, PHK 10.5%, PTY 10.9%, PCN 17.7%, PDI 25.7%) [V counts]. My guess is legacy non-agency RMBS [U].
- Whether muni ratings histories exist under 17g-7. They are free but lagged, and the repo panel holds corporates only.
- Mark-versus-trade lag on the distressed CUSIPs using R2's MSRB trades (fit period only). It is cheap and is the natural B1(d) follow-up; I did not run it, to keep scope.
