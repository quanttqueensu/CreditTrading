## 0. The answer

1. **R2 is far bigger than the catalogue says: 1,146 tables in 81 schemas, 433.1 GB** [V].
   - It holds the licensed history most event ideas need:
     - Compustat (to 2026-06);
     - IBES (to 2026-05-14);
     - Thomson insiders, including Form 144 since 2001 and a 10b5-1 flag;
     - Thomson 13F (to 2025-12-31);
     - SDC M&A (to 2026-07-01) and SDC New Issues (to 2026-07-02);
     - MSRB muni trades (2005–2025);
     - OTC end-of-day prices with all 37 GSE symbols (2011-09-01 → 2026-06-01);
     - FJC bankruptcy filings (to 2026-03-31);
     - **CRSP CIZ daily to 2025-12-31**. `docs/DATA.md`'s "CRSP stops at 2024-12-31"
       describes the legacy file only.
   - **Absent:**
     - Mergent FISD;
     - OptionMetrics;
     - RavenPack;
     - full Audit Analytics (a 1,491-row sample);
     - Capital IQ;
     - TAQ;
     - ratings after 2017;
     - any EDGAR index;
     - Russell membership;
     - a survivorship-free S&P 400/600 membership history. Compustat's table holds today's
       members only: 503/400/602 rows, 0 `thru` dates [V].
   - The mirror is **one snapshot taken 2026-06-26 → 2026-07-03, never refreshed** [V].
     Anything after mid-2026, and anything live, comes from free sources.
2. **The SEC is the one free source that is both deep and clean on terms.** Its privacy
   page says: "Information presented on sec.gov is considered public information and may
   be copied or further distributed by users of the web site without the SEC's
   permission" [V]. Coverage read today [V]:

   | SEC data set | coverage |

   | Financial Statement data sets | January 2009 – September 2026 |
   | Financial Statement and Notes | January 2009 – September 2026 |

   | N-CEN | September 2018 – September 2026 |

3. **Terms walls, beyond CEFConnect and Kalshi:**

   | source | what blocks automated research use |
   |---|---|

   | Nasdaq Reg SHO files | no storage or use without written consent, beyond one personal, non-commercial copy [V] |

   | S&P DJI | pages answer HTTP 403 to automated requests; terms unreadable [V] |
   | FINRA | non-commercial, plus clause (m) [S] |

   **Terms-clean substitutes exist for two of these:**
   - threshold status can be rebuilt from the SEC's FTD file plus shares outstanding;
   - short interest is in R2 (Compustat), under the WRDS licence rather than FINRA's
     website terms.
4. **Post-2023-only:**
   - electronic Form 144 (EDGAR, 2023-04-13);
   - the Form 4 10b5-1 checkbox (filings from 2023-04-01);
   - Item 408(a) plan disclosures (fiscal quarters from 2023-04-01; small companies from
     2023-10-01);
   - the modern Treasury buyback programme (2024–);
   - December Russell reconstitutions (from 2026);
   - structured 13D/G (2024-12-18).

   **Two of these have licensed pre-2023 history in R2**: Thomson's Form 144 table and its
   10b5-1 flag table. Item 408, the buybacks and the December Russell reconstitution have
   none, so they need a forward-only paper protocol (§4).
5. **Build first** (§5):
   - an EDGAR 8-K item and form-event extractor (submissions bulk);
   - the SEC FTD fetcher, with threshold reconstruction;
   - R2 staging extracts for SDC, Thomson insiders, OTC GSE prices and CRSP MF ETF
     holdings;
   - the SEC Financial Statement data sets;
   - the N-PORT bulk data sets.

   EDGAR full-text search needs an `edgar_client.py` host change, and a team-lead decision
   on fair access.

## 5. Fetchers to build, in priority order

Value is ideas served per hour, with terms-clean sources first.

1. **EDGAR form-event and 8-K-items extractor**, `ssx_data_edgar_items.py`.
   - Reads the nightly `submissions.zip` (www.sec.gov, an allowed host) and keeps per
     filing: form, the `items` field, filing date and accession.
   - The SEC header's acceptance time is taken only for the filings an idea uses (§12z).
   - Serves C2, D6, D4, D9, A3, A16, C1, A1.
   - Terms clean. 2004+ for 8-K items.
2. **SEC FTD fetcher with Reg SHO threshold reconstruction**: `cnsfails` files 2004+,
   joined to CRSP CIZ / Compustat shares outstanding. Serves G1 and gives the CEF-crowding
   context. It is the terms-clean replacement for the Nasdaq and NYSE lists, which may be
   used only with written permission.
3. **R2 staging extracts** (licensed outputs, gitignored like the CRSP merger tables):
   - SDC M&A and New Issues;
   - Thomson insiders (table1, form144, rule10b5);
   - OTC GSE prices;
   - CRSP MF holdings diffs for BIZD, PFF, PGX, IJH, IJR, MDY, SPMD;
   - IBES and Compustat earnings dates;
   - CRSP CIZ distributions and delistings.

   These serve A2, A9, CR15, D1, D5, D8, D9, G2, merger arb and spin-offs. No new
   external source is needed.
4. **SEC Financial Statement data sets** (2009+; Notes monthly). Serve D2 and A3, and the
   Item 408 tags forward.
5. **N-PORT bulk data sets** (2019Q4+, quarterly ZIPs). Serve A7, A9, B1, B4 and CR15.
   This is cheaper than the sibling's per-fund XML parse for anything market-wide.
6. **N-CEN data sets** (2018Q3+). Serves A5.
7. **OpenFEMA** declarations (1953+). Serves B2. Each output carries FEMA's attribution
   string.
8. **ALFRED vintages.** Macro event surprises. **Needs a FRED API key**, which is the team
   lead's credential.
9. **Treasury buybacks and auctions** (fiscaldata, keyless). Macro calendar.
10. **EDGAR full-text search.** Serves A6 and C4. **Needs a change to
    `scripts/data/edgar_client.py`** (add `efts.sec.gov` under the same user agent and rate
    limit). It also needs a team-lead ruling, because the desk's own fetcher judged efts a
    user interface rather than an API.
11. **Not to build without permission:** Nasdaq and NYSE threshold lists, EMMA, S&P DJI
    pages, FINRA files beyond what counsel clears.
