## A4 — non-traded and private BDCs listing: lock-up and staggered releases

**Mechanism and loser.**
- At listing, pre-listing holders are barred from selling by underwriter lock-ups or by charter
  transfer restrictions.
- They are then released in tranches on dates the listing filing states in advance. The common
  pattern is one third each at 180, 270 and 365 days.
- A BDC has no creation or redemption, so a release is new supply against a fixed float.
- The named loser is anyone holding the BDC through a release.
- The pattern persists because the dates are contractual and the holders are yield-oriented
  retail who sell when they can.

**Event clock and first point-in-time source.**
- The terms are in the IPO prospectus (497 before about 2020, 424B1 after) or in the listing
  8-K or proxy. The filing date is the date the terms became public (EDGAR acceptance by 17:30 ET).
- Release dates are listing date + N days, so they are known at listing, months ahead.
- CRSP CIZ `securitybegdt` gives the listing date (headers run to 2025-12-31). 20 of 20 matched
  listings have an 8-A12B within 30 days of that date.

**Expression.**
- Short the BDC MOC into the release window, hedged with HYG/BKLN or a long basket of other BDCs.
- BIZD is **not** shortable.
- On today's flags, **12 of the 17 A4 names still trading are shortable and easy to borrow**.
  TSLX, CGBD, BXSL, PSBD and MSDL are not.

**Census** (`ssx_fund2_bdc_listings.py`).
- **Universe and rule:** 292 BDC CIKs (filed N-54A or N-6F, 2008–2025). A listing counts if the
  BDC first traded in 2010–2025 after ≥ 180 days of 10-K/10-Q reporting, and had elected BDC
  status before listing.
- **21 candidates**, of which 20 are confirmed in CRSP. Terra Income Fund 6 was excluded on
  reading: its 8-A12B registered 7.00% Notes due 2026.
- **Sensitivities:** TCPC (2012, prior N-CSR history; 180 days, affiliates 3 years) and FSSL
  (2025: withdrew its election 16 days before listing through a successor CIK; no terms found).
- **Listings by year:** 2011 1, 2014 2, 2017 2, 2018 1, 2019 1, 2020 2, 2021 5, 2024 5, 2025 2.

**Terms.** Each was read in the filing, and the quote is re-verified against the cached text on
every run (`a4_lockup_terms.csv`).

## A5 — adviser and sponsor change of control

**Mechanism.**
- A change of control of the adviser terminates the advisory contract on assignment (1940 Act
  §15(a)(4) [S, not fetched today]).
- So every fund in the family must hold a vote, and holders face a new manager.
- No forced seller is named, so the loser is [U].

**Event clock:** SDC announcement date; then the family's joint proxy (DEF 14A); then the
effective date.

**Census** (`ssx_fund2_offline_counts.py`, `ssx_fund2_sponsor_deals.py`, `ssx_fund2_proxy_sample.py`).

- **Proxies by listed fund CIKs, 2010–2025:**
  - DEF 14A accessions run 316–376 a year, 5,569 in total.
  - 292 of them are joint proxies covering ≥ 5 funds.
  - DEFA14A spikes in 2020 (803) and 2024 (619).
- **Advisory-agreement proposals:** **2 of 30** in a seeded sample (seed 20261019; 95% CI roughly
  1–22%). Both were family adviser changes: Mexico Fund 2014 and the Virtus–AllianzGI funds 2022.
  The frame's "listed" flag lets in a few non-CEFs (Daxor, Schwab Strategic Trust, an mREIT).
- **Sponsor deals from SDC on R2.** The dates are SDC's. The funds touched are listed family CIKs

## A9 — BDC index-tracker demand (BIZD / MVBDC)

**Mechanism.** BIZD must trade MVBDC's quarterly changes. The loser is the tracker's holders, if
the demand is large relative to liquidity.

**Clock.** Quarterly reviews [S]. Implementation was on 2025-12-19 and 2026-06-19, both third
Fridays [S]. marketvector.com returned HTTP 403 to every fetch, so the methodology, the
announcement lead and the caps are **[S]**: a 20% cap from June 2024; $150m market cap and $1m
three-month ADV screens.

**Census** (`ssx_fund2_bizd.py`; CRSP MF holdings, portno 1032741, monthly 2013-07..2025-11).
- **Coverage:** 41 review brackets.
- **Fit window 2014-09..2022-12:** 159 events (adds 20, deletes 21, reweights ≥ 10% 118), so
  **19.3 a year**; adds and deletes about 5 a year.
- **Size:** median trade **$2.04m = 0.39 days of ADV**. Adds 1.24 days, deletes 1.30 days,
  reweights 0.23 days. 24% of events are ≥ 1 day.
- **AUM:** BIZD equity $516m at 2022-12, $1.28bn at 2025-03.
- **Other trackers (CRSP TNA):** BDCZ $48m → $11m. BDCX $28.5m → $6m. VPC $19m → $36m.
  PBDC (active) $6m → $253m. HBDC (BDC bonds) $85m. FBDC $33m. The ETNs are issuer-hedged, so
  their demand is not forced.
- **Caveat:** a CUSIP change (a reverse split, a merger) shows up as a delete plus an add.
- **Not done:** B2/CA8 (the fund-of-CEF analogue) is referenced only.

**Neighbours, risk, counter and verdict.**
- **Neighbours:** I1 (S&P adds, KILL); S3-wrapper.
- **Flags:** BIZD is not shortable; **23 of 34 BDCs are shortable and easy to borrow**.
- **D4 prior:** low if BDC-hedged.
- **Counter:** EVENTS.
- **Verdict: KILLED** (fewer than 30 events a year, and a median under 1 day of ADV).

- **Graveyard:** `raw-flow-z` (D1) tested a vehicle's own flow against its own return. This is a
  cross-vehicle redemption state with a 5% gate, which means queued and unmet demand. The
  difference is real, but so is the D4 risk.
- **Counter:** none until it conditions the book (then CEF).
