## 1. Event cards

### CR15: PFF and preferred-index additions (the one that passes)

- **Mechanism; named loser; why it persists.**
  - PFF ($12.0bn [doc R21]) tracks ICE PHGY. It must hold an issue once ICE adds it.
    ICE adds at the month-end rebalance, using information up to the third business
    day before the last business day [S: ICE Rules & Methodology, 2021-12-26 copy].
  - **The loser is PFF and its holders**, who pay for immediacy at one dated close.
    Peers (PGX, PFFD, PSK) share the loser role [U on their index dates].
  - It persists because the tracker must track, and a $25 issue of $100m+ is not
    worth a large arbitrageur's balance sheet.
- **Event clock; point-in-time source.**
  - The event is the last calendar day of the month.
  - Eligibility can be rebuilt from public data before the cutoff: amount outstanding
    of at least $100m [S: ICE], NYSE or Nasdaq listing (8-A12B), and settlement.
  - In history, the addition month comes from CRSP MF monthly holdings [V] and the
    quarter from N-PORT-P [V].
- **Expression and hedge.**
  - Alpaca: long the added issue at the close, short PFF at the close. PFF is
    shortable and ETB, as are PGX, PSK and PFFD [V, 2026-10-19]. NYSE-listed issues
    map to the official close print; Nasdaq issues do not yet [doc].
  - IBKR: the same, plus shorts in the issue with a locate.
- **Measured counts.**

  | year | N-PORT-P quarterly additions | CRSP MF monthly additions |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2019 | 43 (Q4 only; first public snapshot 2019-09-30) | 285 (inflated by the PHGL → PHGY transition, which ran to 2019-10-31 [S ICE]) |
  | 2020 | 111 | 101 |
  | 2021 | 127 | 143 |
  | 2022 | 47 | 45 |

  - The two sources were not averaged; they agree to within 10% each year in 2020–22.
  - Holdings ranged over 444–517 (CRSP) and 472–510 (N-PORT).
- **Days of ADV.**
  - The ratio is PFF's whole position at the first report after the addition,
    divided by the issue's mean daily share volume (Alpaca SIP, pre-2023). The
    window is the 20 sessions ending at the previous month-end.
  - All mapped additions: **median 2.02 days** (IQR 1.29–4.71); 86.7% at or above
    1 day (n 75).
  - Seasoned additions, with a strictly pre-event window: **4.88 days** (n 22).
  - Issues that listed inside the addition month: **1.79 days** (n 53). Their window
    includes heavy first-days trading, so these figures understate the pressure.
  - N-PORT-mapped additions: 3.37 days (n 4).
- **Capacity.** Mapped additions have a median pre-event ADV of about 220k shares
  (about $5.5m at par). PFF's median position is about 633k shares. A $100k book is
  not capacity-bound here.
- **Borrow.** The issue leg is long only, so no borrow is needed. The hedge is
  ETB [V].
- **Graveyard neighbour.**
  - S3-forced-flow (WATCH, D6) and S3-wrapper (KILLED, D4): index flow, but that
    was the deletion side, in a wrapper.
  - I1 (S&P adds; the effect is about gone).
  - This is not the stale-mark well: the price is the issue's own exchange close.
- **D4 prior: moderate.** A long new issue against PFF keeps duration and call-structure
  mismatch, and a new-issue liquidity premium. The test: alpha t ≥ 3 with PFF, TERM
  (IEF) and credit (HYG−IEF) betas ≤ 0.10.
- **Negative control (H12).**
  - Issues under the $100m floor that list in the same month.
  - Constituents already in the index.
  - Month-ends with no additions.
- **Counter.** EVENTS (a new source: CRSP MF holdings and N-PORT), or NPORT. **Ruling
  needed.**
- **Gate: PASSES.**
- **Expected magnitude: [U].** No study was found (R22, negative search). The forced
  purchase is about 2 days of ADV, and about 5 for seasoned adds. Uncertainty against
  the 32.6bp breakeven is very wide.
- **E·S·F = 3·1·2 = 6.**

### C1: new $25-par issues, grey market to exchange listing

- **Mechanism; named loser.**
  - Retail allottees and flippers sell in the grey-market weeks before listing;
    index and retail buyers arrive at listing.
  - The loser is the early seller who needs liquidity before the exchange symbol
    exists.
- **Event clock; point-in-time source.**
  - The 424B2/B5 final prospectus, filed by the second business day after pricing
    [V: 17 CFR 230.424(b)(2),(5), LII copy].
  - The 8-A12B (exchange registration).
  - The first SIP bar (first exchange trade).
- **Measured counts: the count gate passes.**
  - EDGAR, 2015–25:
    - 8-A12B candidates (non-fund, non-SPAC, preceded within 45 days by a
      424B2/B5) number 119–454 a year.
    - A stratified sample of 88 was read and checked against each 424B's
      denomination clause. 22 are retail $25 paper: precision 25% (Wilson 17–35%).
      Most "Notes due" 8-A12Bs are $1,000 or euro institutional bonds.
    - The estimate is **30–114 a year** (central, by year). 2023 is the low year
      at 30 (range 20–42).
  - Recall on survivors: **0.829** (287 of 346 issues first trading 2016-03 to
    2022-12).
  - Alpaca survivors by first bar (a lower bound; called issues are gone):

    | 2017 | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | 43 | 33 | 70 | 54 | 101 | 32 |

    A further 161 listed issues have no pre-2023 bar, so they listed in 2023 or later.
  - SDC New Issues, US-listed $25 preferreds only, are 37–75 a year in 2015–21 and
    7–13 a year in 2022–25. **SDC undercounts:** 2021 survivors alone (82
    preferreds) exceed SDC's 71. The thinning after 2021 is consistent with the
    librarian's [U]. Reported, not averaged.
- **The pricing-to-listing gap.**
  - 424B to first exchange bar: **median 8 days** (IQR 7–11, n 287).
  - 424B to 8-A12B: median 5 days (n 2,719).
- **First-month volume, pre-2023.** The median of each issue's median daily volume
  in its first 21 sessions ran 30.8k–119k shares by year, about $0.77m–$2.97m at
  par [V].
- **The grey leg is unreachable at Alpaca.**
  - Alpaca's 18 active OTC rows named preferred or notes are all post-default names
    (Q-suffix, FRC, SBNY).
  - A live case: IIPR 9.25% Series B ($25 liquidation preference). Prospectus
    2026-10-09 (accession 0001104659-26-115075), "trading … within 30 days"; 8-A12B
    filed 2026-10-14. It has **no Alpaca asset, active (14,412) or inactive
    (19,165), on 2026-10-19** [V].
  - IBKR does not accept opening orders in OTC "Grey Market" microcaps [S: IBKR
    knowledge-base summary; pages returned 403]. Whether a new preferred's
    pre-listing line carries that designation is [U].
- **Graveyard neighbour.** E2 (convertible issuance reversal) and K6 (SEO reversal)
  in the corp catalogue.
- **D4 prior: moderate to high** (a new-issue liquidity premium).
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Gate: KILL on access.** The variant that buys at listing does not need the grey
  leg, but it is a different, long-only new-issue-premium trade, which is CR15's
  neighbour.
- **E·S·F = 3·1·0 = 0.**

### C4: fixed-to-floating preferreds at the float or first-call date

- **Mechanism; loser.** Holders who priced to call are wrong when the issuer does not
  call at a SOFR-plus reset.
- **Measured counts.**
  - Name tags among tradable listed issues: **83 fixed-to-float or reset**, 15 pure
    floating; **10 of the 83 shortable**.
  - The prospectus sample of 20 tagged issues (424B, public) gave 10 dates:
    - 5 first-call dates already passed with the issue still listed, so they
      floated;
    - **3 fall in 2026–30** (2026-11-15, 2027-01-30, 2028-04-30);
    - 2 fall after 2030;
    - 10 were not located.
  - Recall of the name tag: 1 of 14 untagged preferreds read is a reset issue
    (WFC.PRD, "reset period"): 7% (Wilson 1–31%), so roughly 23 extra floaters
    (4–100).
  - The central estimate is (83 + 23) × 3/10 over 5 years, **about 6 float dates a
    year**.
  - The ceiling of about 37 a year needs the upper recall bound **and** an event for
    every issue inside the window. The sample contradicts that: half the located
    dates have already passed.
- **Floated versus called, 2018–22: not established.** Alpaca's corporate-actions
  feed returned 16 redemption rows for 2020–25, all in 2023 and none on a ".PR"
  symbol, so it holds no preferred call history.
- **D4 prior: high** (a short-rate and TERM bet).
- **Neighbour.** Carry closed by ruling; J6 pull-to-par (PARK).
- **Gate: KILL.**
