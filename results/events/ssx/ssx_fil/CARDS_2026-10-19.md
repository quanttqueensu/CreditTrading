counter moves (CEF 52, GAMMA 0, EVENTS 0, XASSET 4). No 2023+ price or return
series was read. Pre-event ADV uses events ≤ 2022-12-31 and CRSP `dsf` rows < 2023,
and the scripts raise otherwise. No `cef_universe.csv` name had a return computed.

  `contrib_ceo_turnover`.

**EDGAR live requests: 451 of 1,200**, every one logged in `edgar_live_requests.jsonl`
and spaced at 1.5 s. Labels: **[V]** measured here or fetched today; **[S]** sourced,

**The CIK→permno link** is Compustat CIK × CCM, primary links, dated. It needs no
historical tickers and no name matching.
- **Recall check:** 1,654 NT events in 2018–22 were filed by CIKs that are
  exchange-listed today.
  - 1,170 of them link.
  - 467 do not link because their CCM link starts later: they were OTC at the
    time and uplisted afterwards.
  - Only 17 events (6 CIKs) are absent from CCM entirely.
- **Listed common** = shrcd 10/11 on NYSE, NYSE American, Nasdaq or Arca.
- **ADV** = mean |prc|×vol over sessions t−21..t−2.

## Headline: the acceptance clock

**A correction to `docs/REFERENCES.md` §12z and event engine §4, which say "the error
is always late".**
- In 250 NT filing headers sampled for D4 (2015–25), the submissions API's
  `acceptanceDateTime` differs from the header as follows [V]:
  - equal on 59;
  - one UTC offset **late** on 58;
  - one UTC offset **early** on **133**.
- In FSDS 2022q1 (5,991 10-Q/10-Ks), against FSDS `accepted` [V]:
  - equal on 2,184;
  - late on 2,092;
  - early on 1,715.
- The early cases put acceptance before EDGAR opens (an 08:52 ET header reads as
  04:52), or contradict the 17:30 filing-date rule.
- **So the API can leak.** A 17:16 filing reads as 13:16, before the close.
- Keep `observed_at = max(header acceptance, 06:00 ET on the filing date)`. For
  research, use "filing date ≤ t" at the 22:00 decision.

## Event cards

### D4 — Late-filing notices (Form 12b-25: NT 10-K / NT 10-Q)

- **Mechanism / loser / persistence.**
  - A notice that the 10-K or 10-Q is late is bad news.
  - Bartov & Konchitchki (*Accounting Horizons*, 2,115 firms) [S, NYU Stern summary]
    report:
    - a significant drop at the NT;
    - more than 2% over 5 days for late 10-Qs filed within the extension, and more
      than 4% when the extension is also missed;
    - declines that "can continue for months".
  - The loser is the holder who reads the notice as clerical. It persists because the
    notice is short-heavy, in small caps and hard to borrow.
- **Clock / PIT.**
  - The NT is due within one business day of the deadline, so "no report by the
    deadline" is known a day earlier from absence (see D9).
  - **72.7% of listed notices are accepted after 16:00 ET** (n 150, 2015–22;
    CI 65–79%) [V]: 12.7% pre-open, 14.7% in market hours, 8.7% after 17:30
    (next-day filing date).
  - Our t+1 MOC is the first close after publication. The t+1 open takes the
    initial reaction, so only drift after that is capturable.
- **Expression.** Short at the t+1 MOC, hedged with IWM or a sector SPDR (ETB [V
  catalogue]).
- **Measured** [V, `ssx_fil_d4_nt.py`]:
  - Notices by year 2008→2025: 5,962; 5,508; 4,887; 4,877; 4,469; 4,242; 3,914;
    3,564; 3,323; 2,941; 2,632; 2,413; 1,778; 2,583; 2,617; 2,901; 2,632; 1,882.
  - 63,125 notices from 10,265 issuers in all. The master index and the submissions
    archive agree to the filing.
  - Listed common 2010–22: 4,833 of 44,240 notices (10.9%). Median ADV $430k; 36%
    have ADV ≥ $1m.
  - **Liquid (ADV ≥ $1m) events 2010–22: 1,718** (75–284 a year), 1,361 issuer-years,
    553 distinct filing dates. Notices cluster at the deadlines.
  - BDC notices: 169 in 2008–25, 1–17 a year.
- **Capacity.** At 5% of one session's ADV, the median listed notice ($430k ADV)
  carries a $21k position.
- **Borrow** [V, 2026-10-19]:
  - Of the 2025 notice filers listed today and found at Alpaca, **109 of 358 are
    shortable (30.4%, CI 25.9–35.4%)**. Every shortable one is ETB.
  - Issuers with a liquid notice in 2018–22 that are still listed: **203 of 289
    shortable (70.2%, CI 64.7–75.2%)**. This read is survivor-skewed.
- **Graveyard neighbour.** G1, the reverse-split short, was killed on measured borrow.
  Also C5's session-drift lesson: declare a same-day non-NT control.
- **D4 prior.** Medium-high. A distressed small-cap short loads on size and
  profitability (RMW).
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.**
  - Event count: < 250 events → **passes** (1,718).
  - Borrow: < half shortable today → **fails** on the brief's denominator, the
    2025 filers (30%).
  - **KILL as written.** The team lead could rule the liquid subset the right
    denominator (70%). It cannot be measured for 2025 without 2023+ prices.
- **E·S·F 3·1·1 = 3.**

### D6 — 8-K governance red flags (4.01, 4.02, unplanned 5.02)

- **Mechanism / loser.**
  - Auditor resignations draw negative returns (Shu 2000 [S]).
  - Restatements draw about −9% over 2 days (Palmrose–Richardson–Scholz 2004,
    403 restatements, 1995–99 [S]).
  - Abrupt CEO/CFO exits signal hidden trouble.
  - The loser is the holder who waits for the 10-K.
- **Clock.** Item 4.01 must be filed within 4 business days. The share filed after
  the close is 70% for 4.01, 80% for 4.02 and 66% for 5.02 (n 60 / 40 / 80) [V].
- **Measured** [V, `ssx_fil_d6_8k.py`, full census of every 8-K, not a sample]:

  | item | filings/yr 2010–22 | liquid events 2010–22 | 2025 filers shortable today |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 4.01 | 582–1,303 | **1,467** (1,192 issuers) | **220 of 411 = 53.5%** (48.7–58.3) |
  | 4.02 | 96–387 (840 in 2021) | **621**; 444 without 2021 | **37 of 63 = 58.7%** (46.4–70.0) |
  | 5.02 | 11,989–14,828 | 77,107 (raw) | 2,538 of 3,307 = 76.7% |

  - The 2021 spike is the SPAC/de-SPAC warrant-restatement wave, one common cause.
- **Text classes** (declared regex, listed issuers 2015–22) [V]:
  - **4.01:** dismissal 66.7% (CI 54–77), **resignation or declined to stand
    23.3% (14–35)**, audit-firm merger 1.7%.
    - That implies **≈342 liquid auditor resignations in 2010–22 (CI 212–520)** [E].
  - **4.02:** 0 of 40 cite auditor notice under (b), CI 0–8.8%.
  - **5.02:** CEO/CFO departure in 7.5% (3.5–15.4%); unplanned in **5.0% (2.0–12.2%)**.
    - That implies ≈300 a year among liquid filers [E, wide].
    - The R2 forced-CEO list (`contrib_ceo_turnover`, all rows forced) holds 334
      events in 2015–21, about 48 a year among large firms.
- **Planned vs unplanned (the proposed rule).** A departure is unplanned if:
  - the sentence names the CEO or CFO together with a departure verb;
  - no retirement word appears;
  - and it has an interim successor, "effective immediately" or a termination.
  - Gentry et al. (*SMJ* 2021; open data on Zenodo [S]) is the reference to
    validate it against.
- **Expression.** Short at the t+1 MOC, hedged with a sector SPDR.
- **Capacity.** Liquid by construction.
- **Graveyard neighbour.** G1 (borrow) and E1-G1a (short-leg feasibility).
- **D4 prior.** Medium-high (quality and size).
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.** **PASS.** Every flag clears 250 liquid events. Borrow is ≥ half for
  all three, but the 4.01 interval straddles 50%.
- **E·S·F 3·1·2 = 6.**

### D9 — Report timing ("time will tell")

- **Mechanism.** Johnson & So (*JFQA* 2018, 53(6)) [V abstract]: firms that
  *schedule* later-than-expected announcements report worse news, and prices do
  not reflect it until the announcement.
- **Our free analogue: absence.**
  - Expected date = last year's same-form, same-period filing date, shifted by the
    change in period end.
  - A **late event fires at 22:00 ET on the 5th weekday after the expected date if
    no report has a filing date ≤ that day**. The 17:30 rule puts evening filings on
    the next day, so this is point-in-time.
- **Which clock.** It is the 8-K 2.02 release, not the 10-Q.
  - 2.02 precedes or coincides with the report in 49–68% of firm-quarters, rising
    over time.
  - Median 2 days ahead (IQR 0–13).
  - Against Compustat RDQ: same day on 82.9%, within ±1 day on 91.8% (n 117,416
    10-Qs, 2010–22) [V].
- **Measured** [V, `ssx_fil_d9_timing.py`]:
  - 440,344 matched firm-quarters in 2009–25; a mean 5.3k–7.6k filers a quarter.
  - ≥ 5 weekdays late: 7.1–11.3% a year. ≥ 5 early: 8.4–14.0%.
  - **Liquid late events 2010–22: mean 368 a year, minimum 207 (2010)**; 3,963
    issuer-years. On the 2.02 clock: mean 594 a year.
  - 82.7% of liquid late 10-Qs still came within 45 days, so they were late against
    habit, not against any deadline. 14.8% had an NT in between (the D4 overlap).
  - BDC late reports: 1–23 a year.
  - FSDS cross-check: filed date agrees on 99.97%. Period agrees on 94.1% [V]; the
    gap is plausibly FSDS rounding the period to month-end [U].
- **Confounds.**
  - Filer-status deadline changes.
  - 2020 (622 liquid late events): COVID filing relief, a common cause.
- **Expression.** Short late reporters (or long early ones) from the signal day
  through the release, hedged with SPY or a sector SPDR.
- **Borrow.** Not measured for late reporters.
- **Graveyard neighbour.** PEAD and the earnings-announcement premium; C5's session
  drift.
- **D4 prior.** Medium. It could be earnings momentum or quality in costume.
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.** < 250 liquid late events a year → **PASS** on the mean. 12 of 13
  years clear; 2010 does not (207).
- **E·S·F 3·1·2 = 6.** S rises to 2 if the paper's magnitude is ≥ 1% when read.

### D2 — BDC earnings with NAV printed at earnings (parked B5)

- **Measured** [V, `ssx_fil_d2_bdc.py`]:
  - 345 CIKs have elected BDC status (N-54A).
  - BDC 10-Q/10-K reports by year: 161 (2008) → 667 (2025); listed reports 96–191 a
    year.
  - 2.02 releases: 98–377 a year.
  - **XBRL `NetAssetValuePerShare` on the report's own accession and period:**
    - 0.0% in every year 2008–18; 0.3–1.1% in 2019–21; 10.9% in 2022;
    - **83.5% / 91.8% / 90.9% in 2023 / 24 / 25**; listed BDCs 97.7% and 100% in
      2023–24.
    - The `isXBRL` flag mirrors this (≤ 4% before 2022).
    - Consistent with BDCs entering XBRL only under the 2020 closed-end offering
      reform [U on the rule's text].
  - **Lags:**
    - 10-Q: median 40 days after quarter-end (p10 34, p90 45).
    - 10-K: median 70 days.
    - Listed 2.02 release: median 37 days. 83% of reports have a 2.02 at or before
      them, a median 0 days earlier.
    - Release sample: **17 of 20 print NAV per share (CI 64–95%)**, all as text.
      15 were after the close, 4 pre-open.
- **Borrow.** 29 of 47 listed 2025 BDC filers are shortable (61.7%) [V].
- **Graveyard neighbour.** `single-fund-PD` (staleness) and CR14.
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.** XBRL coverage < 80% of BDC-quarters → **KILL for any fit window.**
  - 2012–22 coverage is 1.6% (2.1% for listed BDCs).
  - Coverage passes only in 2023+, which is the sealed time holdout.
  - History needs NAV read from text by the event engine.
- **E·S·F 2·1·1 = 2.**

### D8 — S&P MidCap 400 / SmallCap 600 changes

- **Data fact** [V]: Compustat `idxcst_his` holds current members only. The 400 has
  exactly 400 rows and the 600 has 602, all open-ended. It cannot count changes.
  - Additions by today's members alone, 2023/24/25: 400: 48/35/32; 600: 80/60/63. That
    is a lower bound of 128/95/95.
- **S&P DJI releases on PR Newswire** [V, fetched 2026-10-19]:
  - 55 releases naming the 400 or 600 in the title between 2023-12-19 and 2025-07-01,
    about 36 a year. All were sent 17:30–19:17 ET, after the close.
  - The 2024 quarterly rebalances added 13 + 20 + 23 + 8 = **64** names, of which at
    least 17 moved between the 400 and 600 (June not split).
  - Seven 2024–25 ad hoc releases averaged about 1.6 additions.
  - That gives **≈117 additions in 2024 [E]** and **about 36 distinct announcement
    dates**.
- **Gap from announcement to the index funds' close (E−1)** [V, 12 effective groups]:
  - ad hoc: 2, 3, 3, 3, 3, 3, 4, 5 sessions;
  - quarterly: 9, 10, 10, 10;
  - **median 3.** Our t+1 MOC precedes the index trade in **12 of 12**.
- **Implied passive demand** (today's members added 2015–22; share = indexed assets
  / index cap, both [S]: 14.7% and 8.85%):
  - **400: 15.1 days of ADV** (IQR 10.4–24.3; n 158; median ADV $41.7m);
  - **600: 9.7 days** (6.0–17.5; n 196; ADV $11.2m);
  - 100% / 99.5% of these have ADV ≥ $1m.
- **Prior.** The S&P 500 effect fell to 0.8% in the 2010s (Greenwood–Sammon NBER
  w30748 [V abstract]). Migrations from the MidCap offset tracker demand [V], and the
  same decline is reported for other S&P and Russell indices [S].
- **Expression.** Long the addition at t+1 MOC, short MDY or IJR (ETB [V catalogue]),
  exit at the E−1 close.
- **Graveyard neighbour.** I1 (S&P 500, killed) and S3-forced-flow (WATCH).
- **D4 prior.** Low (hedged, a few sessions).
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.** < 100 changes a year, or median gap < 2.
  - **Marginal PASS** on names (≈117 [E]; lower bound 95) with a gap of 3.
  - **Fails** if independence is counted by announcement date (about 36).
  - Needs a ruling.
- **E·S·F 2·1·3 = 6.**

### D7 — Customer–supplier contagion (feasibility only)

- **Mechanism.**
  - Suppliers' prices fall significantly around a customer's distress and filing
    (Hertzel–Li–Officer–Rodgers, *JFE* 2008 [V abstract]).
  - Customer momentum earns more than 150bp a month (Cohen–Frazzini, *JF* 2008
    [V abstract]).
- **Measured** [V, `ssx_fil_d7_chain.py`]:
  - 8-K item 1.03 filings 2010–22: 40–198 a year; 2023–25: 156, 156, 87.
  - Filers with assets ≥ $1bn (XBRL `Assets`, 2011–22): 7–41 a year, **mean 17.75**.
    Listed ones: 0–14, mean 5.25.
  - **Sample of the 10 large listed filers of 2020–22:**
    - 5 of 10 have at least one supplier naming them in Compustat `seg_customer`
      (window −2 years to +1 year, read by a human). One false positive (Frontier
      Airlines) was removed, and one was missed through a name change (Oasis/Chord).
    - 8 supplier links in all, **0.8 per bankruptcy**.
- **Link routes.**
  - Compustat `seg_customer` exists on R2 but **only from datadate 2020-06-30**
    (2,600–3,000 suppliers a year name companies).
  - The free route is FSDS dimensional facts: 295 of 4,862 10-Ks in 2022q1 (6.1%)
    tag a MajorCustomers member, some with generic labels.
  - Extraction from 10-K text needs the event engine.
- **Estimate.** About 18 large bankruptcies a year × 0.8 links ≈ **14 linked supplier
  events a year [E]**. It is an upper bound, since supplier liquidity was not checked.
- **Counter.** EVENTS.
- **Kill gate.** < 30 a year → **KILL** for the bankruptcy form. The continuous
  customer-momentum form is a different trial, with links in R2 only from 2020.
- **E·S·F 1·2·1 = 2.**

## What could not be established

- **The literature's magnitudes.** No full text of Bartov–Konchitchki, Johnson–So,
  Shu or Palmrose was read. Every S cell is an abstract or a summary.
- **Liquidity of 2025 filers.** Their shortability by liquidity is unmeasurable
  without 2023+ prices.
- **Classifier error rates.** The 4.01/4.02/5.02 classifiers have no hand-coded
  validation beyond the reads above.
- **D8's full count.** The change count is an estimate. The free source (PR
  Newswire) covers releases, not every index notice. 2010–22 announcement dates were
  not collected.
- **D7 supplier liquidity.** The listing and liquidity of D7's suppliers were not
  checked.
- **FSDS segment history.** Whether the FSDS `segments` column covers quarters before
  2022 is unverified.
- **XBRL rule text.** The rule that brought BDCs into XBRL is unverified.
- **Paper borrow.** Alpaca paper shows shortable ⇔ ETB, with one exception. HTB costs
  at a live broker are unknown.
