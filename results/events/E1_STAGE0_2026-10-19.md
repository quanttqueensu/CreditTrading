    The file is byte-identical in five sibling worktrees.

    included; the assembly raises otherwise. Two pulls of one extract (2026-10-11 and
    2026-10-12) were also checked equal, row for row.
- **EDGAR.**
  - `results/events/e1/_cache_e1/edgar_union/` is a symlinked union of the 13 sibling
    worktrees' EDGAR caches, taking the newest copy of each path.

    that the offline name table loads. No deal's dating window reaches 2026.

    the `research-edgar` worktree. The dates file's SHA-1 is identical in three worktrees.
    Both files are gitignored.
